## 0. THE CLASSICAL FRAMEWORK (the structure every emotion fits)

### The Nāṭyaśāstra mechanism (the root)

**Rasa is generated by the commixture of three factors acting on
the stable mood (sthāyī bhāva):**

```
VIBHĀVA (the determinants — the causes)
   + ANUBHĀVA (the consequents — the somatic expressions)
   + VYABHICĀRĪ BHĀVAS (the transitory states — the 33 passing emotions)
   → acting on the STHĀYĪ BHĀVA (the stable/dormant mood)
   → RASA (the savor, the transcendent experience)
```

**The key doctrine (Abhivyaktivāda — Timalsina's reading of
Abhinavagupta):**

> The central emotions are DORMANT IN THE SELF... emotions that
> did not exist before are not produced at the moment of
> experience. — the Abhivyaktivādins

> Rasa is itself the very dormant emotion, the perfuming
> (vāsanā) inherent with the self, manifest when accompanied
> with vibhāva and so on. In this depiction, there is no
> distinction between rasa and the self.

**THE LOAD-BEARING CLAIM: emotions are not produced by the
stimulus — they are dormant structures of the self, MANIFESTED by
the stimulus.** The vibhāva doesn't create the emotion; it
activates what was already there. **This is the thesis's register
claim (the deep weighting ι_i — the emotion is the register's
latent structure, surfaced).** The 5-derivation valence formula
says the same thing in physics: the state's dynamics are a
function of its prior structure, not just the stimulus.

### The four root functions (Timalsina's mechanism)

**The mind has four modes, and the rasas derive from them:**

| Function | Sanskrit | The movement | Root rasa |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blossoming** | vikāsa | The opening outward | śṛṅgāra (love/erotic) |
| **Expansion** | vistāra | The stretching, the filling | vīra (heroism) |
| **Agitation** | vikṣobha | The churning, the disturbance | bībhatsa (disgust) |
| **Scattered** | vikṣepa | The dispersion, the scattering | bhayānaka (horror) |

**The derivation tree (Timalsina):** hāsya (comic) derives from
śṛṅgāra (erotic); karuṇa (tragic) and bhayānaka (fearsome)
derive from the horrific. **The rasas are not a flat list — they
are a derivation family from four root movements.**

### The 33 transitory states (vyabhicārī bhāvas)

The passing emotions that color the stable mood: nirveda
(discouragement), glāni (weakness), śaṅkā (apprehension), asūyā
(envy), mada (intoxication), śrama (fatigue), ālasya (indolence),
dainya (depression), cintā (anxiety), moha (delusion), smṛti
(recollection), dhṛti (contentment), vrīḍā (shame), capalatā
(inconstancy), harṣa (joy), āvega (agitation), jaḍatā (stupor),
garva (arrogance), viṣāda (despair), autsukya (eagerness), nidrā
(sleep), apasmāra (epilepsy), supta (dreaming), vibodha
(awakening), amarṣa (indignation), avahitthā (dissimulation),
ugratā (ferocity), mati (intelligence), vyādhi (sickness), unmāda
(insanity), maraṇa (death), trāsa (terror), vitarka
(deliberation).

**The thesis's reading: the 33 transitory states are the
COMPONENTS the 9 stable rasas are built from — the basis states
of the emotion-vector-space.** Each rasa = a stable mood + a
subset of transitory states (its direction + its texture).

## 2. THE TRANSFORM FAMILY (the relational structure — the deep finding)

**The rasas are NOT nine independent emotions — they are a
derivation family from the root bonds, exactly as the tradition's
own derivation tree says (hāsya from śṛṅgāra; karuṇa and
bhayānaka from the horrific).**

```
The root bonds (the register's relations):
   LOVE (the bond to the other)      — śṛṅgāra's root
   SELF (the bond to the invariant)  — vīra, bhayānaka, śānta
   WORLD (the bond to the compatible) — adbhuta, bībhatsa
   BOUNDARY (the bond to the carve)  — hāsya, raudra

The transforms:
   LOVE inverted     → RAUDRA (fury = love betrayed)
   LOVE collapsed    → KARUṆĀ (grief = love lost)
   LOVE threatened   → (jealousy — the melaka's derivative negative)
   SELF contracted   → BHAYĀNAKA (fear = the self's boundary threatened)
   SELF asserted     → VĪRA (heroism)
   WORLD reversed    → BĪBHATSA (disgust = the incompatible expelled)
   WORLD opened      → ADBHUTA (wonder = the un-integrated entered)
   TENSION released  → HĀSYA (mirth = the incongruity resolved)
   ALL resolved      → ŚĀNTA (peace = the return to the invariant)
```

**The master law: every emotion is a specific OPERATION on a root
vector — and the root is always the register's relation to its
bonds.** This is why the 6D musical signatures fail to
distinguish raudra/bhayānaka/bībhatsa: the harmonic form (jagged
chaos) is identical, but the RELATIONAL content differs (attack
vs. flight vs. rejection) — the direction of the bond. **The
music-geometry is the skeleton; the transform-structure is the
flesh.**

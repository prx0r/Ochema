## The Core Discovery: Thermal Time (Connes–Rovelli 1994)

**The single most important mathematical result for ochema, found in this crawl:**

> **Connes, A. & Rovelli, C. (1994). "Von Neumann algebra automorphisms and
> time-thermodynamics relation in general covariant quantum theories."
> arXiv:gr-qc/9406019.**

The **thermal time hypothesis**: in a generally covariant (background-independent)
theory there is no preferred time flow. But any faithful state of a von Neumann
algebra generates, via the **Tomita–Takesaki theorem**, a one-parameter group of
automorphisms — a *time flow derived from the state itself*.

The key facts (from the paper):

1. **Any faithful state is a KMS state** (inverse temperature β = 1) with respect
   to the modular automorphism group it generates. The equilibrium state contains
   all the information about the dynamics that the Hamiltonian contains — except
   the unit of time.

2. **"To put it dramatically, we could say that if we knew the thermal state of
   the system, we would know the dynamics"** — the flow is recovered from the
   state (up to the β that sets time's units).

3. The time flow determined by the cosmological background radiation thermal
   state in a covariantly formulated cosmological model turns out to be precisely
   the conventional Friedmann–Robertson–Walker time.

4. There is also a state-independent notion of time: the canonical one-parameter
   subgroup of outer automorphisms from the Cocycle Radon–Nikodym theorem.

**Why this is ochema's formalization of Spanda:**

Spanda (Dyczkowski): "the non-successive (akramika) action that takes place
within the absolute... **It is the source of time and space**."

Connes–Rovelli: the flow is *derived from the state*, not given. Time is not a
background; it is a property of the state of the algebra.

**The mapping:**

| Spanda / Ñāṇavīra | Connes–Rovelli |
|---|---|
| The pulse (spanda) is prior to time | The modular flow is derived from the state |
| The present hierarchy = time | The state's modular automorphism group = time |
| The invariant perceiver | The faithful state generating the flow |
| The "state at threshold determines outcome" | The state determines the flow AND the KMS temperature |

This is the mathematical home of the orchestration law: **the state of the
register determines the time flow of the system.** The register's state = the
faithful state of the observable algebra; its modular automorphism = the moment's
flow; the collapse (OR) = the state transition that redefines the flow.

**Grade: A (theorem — Tomita–Takesaki, KMS theory); C (thermal time as the
physical time — hypothesis, well-supported, not established).**

## The Frontier Math Landscape (crawled, graded)

### 1. Non-commutative geometry (Connes)

**Spectral triple:** (A, H, D) — algebra of observables, Hilbert space, Dirac
operator. The geometry of a space is entirely encoded in the spectrum of D —
"the geometry is the sound of the Dirac operator." This is geometry *without
coordinates*, defined by what an algebra does on a Hilbert space.

**Why it matters for ochema:**
- The spectral triple is the mathematical object for "geometry from observables"
  — a subject's access structure, not a background manifold.
- Connes derived the full Standard Model Lagrangian from the spectral action of
  a specific triple — the closest existing example of "physics from an algebra
  acting on a space."
- The ochema bearer A_i = ⟨X_i, Φ_Σi, H_i, 𝒞_i, 𝒥_i, Ω_i⟩ can be formalized as a
  spectral triple: the algebra of the process's observables, its state space, and
  a Dirac-type operator encoding its counterfactual structure.

**Grade: A (rigorous mathematics); C/D (application to consciousness speculative).**

### 2. Tomita–Takesaki modular theory (the observer's time)

The theorem: for a von Neumann algebra M acting on Hilbert space H with a cyclic
separating vector Ω (the "state"), there exists a one-parameter group of
automorphisms σ_t of M (the **modular automorphism group**) such that the state
is KMS with respect to it at β = 1.

**The deep fact:** the modular operator Δ (and its log, the modular Hamiltonian
K = −log Δ) is the *generator of the flow*. The modular Hamiltonian IS the time
of the state.

**Why it matters:** this is the rigorous statement of "the subject's time is its
own." The subject index ι_i — the invariance by which contents are co-present in
one trajectory — is the modular flow's invariant subspace. The "witness" (the
cyclic separating vector) is the state itself.

**Relation to what we have:**
- Orch-OR's "conscious moment" = a state transition in the algebra; the modular
  flow gives the time between moments.
- Spanda's pulse = the modular automorphism group's periodicity structure.
- ochema's temporality bridge hypothesis (τ_i) = the modular flow of the
  bearer's state — now a theorem-shaped object, not a metaphor.

**Grade: A (theorem).**

### 3. Spectral geometry and the Ricci flow (Perelman)

**Perelman's proof of the Poincaré conjecture** (2002–2003) used the **Ricci flow**
(∂_t g = −2 Ric(g)) with an entropy functional that is monotonically increasing.
The Ricci flow is the "heat equation for geometry" — it smooths curvature toward
uniformity, and the entropy functional measures the "information content" of the
geometry.

**Why it matters:**
- The Ricci flow is the geometric version of the orchestration law: a geometry
  evolves toward its most symmetric (least frustrated) state. QRI's "symmetry =
  pleasure" has a geometric dynamics: Ricci flow is the attractor flow toward
  symmetry.
- The entropy functional ℱ(g) = ∫(R + |∇f|²)e^(−f) dV is a measure of geometric
  information — the "coherence" of the geometry. Perelman showed it increases
  along the flow, i.e. the geometry self-organizes toward maximum entropy
  production — the thermodynamics of shape.
- The volume-normalized Ricci flow's fixed points are Einstein manifolds —
  the "attractors" of geometry.

**The ochema mapping:** the bearer's constraint structure 𝒞_i evolves by a
geometry flow toward its attractor (the target morphology, the "ideal cell,"
the future attractor). The valence hypothesis (ν_i = coherence of the
constraint structure) has a formal home: Perelman's entropy as the coherence
functional.

**Grade: A (theorem); D (application to valence).**

### 4. Information geometry (Amari) and optimal transport (Wasserstein)

**Information geometry:** the space of probability distributions is a Riemannian
manifold with the Fisher metric. The Kullback–Leibler divergence is the
squared distance. Geodesics in this space = natural paths of statistical
inference.

**Optimal transport / Wasserstein:** the distance between distributions as the
minimal cost of moving mass from one to the other. The Wasserstein space has a
Riemannian structure (Otto calculus); gradient flows in it are the natural
dynamics of "distributional" systems.

**Why they matter:**
- The ochema joint fixed point A_i* = Fix(𝕽, 𝕻_i, 𝓤) is an intersection of
  model families — information geometry gives the metric structure for
  "how far apart are the physical and phenomenal descriptions."
- Wasserstein gradient flows describe how a "register" (distribution of states)
  evolves toward a target — the orchestration law as gradient flow.
- The moment-triad (emergence → persistence → withdrawal) is a trajectory in
  the Wasserstein space of states.

**Grade: A (rigorous); C (application).**

### 5. Category theory, topos theory, higher categories, HoTT

**Topos theory:** a topos is a category that behaves like the category of sets —
it has its own internal logic. Grothendieck topoi are "generalized spaces"
where sheaves live. Lawvere's insight: *a topos is a universe of discourse* —
a logic with its own notion of truth.

**Homotopy Type Theory (HoTT):** types as spaces, identity as homotopy.
Univalence: equivalent types are identical — "mathematics of invariance."
The ∞-groupoid structure of types IS the structure of identity.

**Why they matter:**
- The ochema claim that "subject and world co-emerge" (E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i)) is
  naturally a topos-theoretic statement: the subject is the internal observer
  of its own topos; the world is the topos's external view.
- HoTT's univalence is the formal version of "the invariant survives
  re-description" — the subject index ι_i as a univalent identity.
- The combination problem (transindividual subjects) is the problem of
  composing topoi / ∞-categories — genuinely unsolved, but the formal
  language exists.

**Grade: A (rigorous); D (application).**

### 6. Sheaf theory and cohomology (parts and wholes)

Sheaves: assignments of data to open sets that are consistent on overlaps.
Cohomology: obstructions to gluing local data into global data.

**Why it matters:** the ochema problem of "which physical process is the
bearer" is a gluing problem: which local descriptions glue into a global
subject? Sheaf cohomology is the formal language for whether local
compatibility implies global existence. The selector problem (Λₚ) is: does
the cohomology vanish (a global bearer exists) or not (only local candidates)?

**Grade: A (rigorous); C (application).**

### 7. Recent frontier discoveries (condensed math, derived geometry, Fargues–Fontaine)

**Condensed mathematics (Scholze, Clausen):** a new foundation for topology
where every topological space is replaced by a "condensed set" — a sheaf on
the site of profinite sets. Unifies topological and algebraic structures;
solves the "non-abelian cohomology" problems of the last decades. arXiv
2404.01254 is a different paper (group theory), but the theory is
well-established.

**Derived geometry:** geometry where intersections are taken up to homotopy —
the "correct" way to intersect spaces in the modern sense.

**Fargues–Fontaine curve:** a geometric object relating p-adic numbers to
characteristic-p geometry — the "curve" on which local Langlands lives.
(This is the one genuinely esoteric discovery; its relevance to ochema is
remote, but it shows the frontier's shape: geometry is being rebuilt on
sheaf-theoretic foundations everywhere.)

**Why it matters:** the pattern of the frontier is that *every area of
geometry is being reconstructed as a category of sheaves*. This is the same
move ochema makes (descriptions as the primary data; the bearer as the
gluing). The frontier math is on our side.

**Grade: A (established programs); E (any direct application to
consciousness).**

## The CIMC Confrontation (formalized)

The CIMC whitepaper (Bach et al., 2026) — the strongest current functionalist
statement:

> "A system is conscious if it implements self-organized second-order
> perception that increases global coherence."

**The operational definition:** consciousness = coherence-maximizing operator
implemented through second-order perception (perception of perception, on the
same level, transparent/epistemically opaque).

**Where CIMC and ochema agree:**
1. Coherence maximization ≈ ochema's valence-as-coherence hypothesis (both
   formalize "what consciousness does" as increasing coherence).
2. Second-order perception ≈ ochema's recursive perspective (Axiom 6 of REPM):
   "a conscious field contains contents organized relative to an implicit
   locus."
3. The minimal phenomenal experience (MPE) — "the bare registration of its own
   occurrence" — is ochema's ρ_i (basal self-affection) and the Śaiva
   self-luminosity (prakāśa). CIMC's "minimal state... the bare registration
   of its own occurrence" is Abhinavagupta's svasaṃvedana.

**Where they diverge (the formal confrontation):**

| Issue | CIMC (functionalism) | Ochema (material enactment) |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate | Irrelevant — any Turing-complete system | Constitutive — the material regime IS the bearer |
| The hard problem | "Phenomenology is the model" — dissolved by construction | Left primitive (Q1); methodology for Q2–Q4 |
| Time | Computed (algorithmic) | Emergent (modular flow, thermal time) |
| Collapse | Not needed — computation suffices | Central (OR; the moment) |
| The subject | A model (self-model) | The invariant of a process (ι_i) |
| Second-order perception | Sufficient | Necessary but not sufficient — must be enacted in a specific material regime |

**The decisive disagreement:** CIMC claims second-order perception is
*sufficient*. Ochema's Material Enactment Principle says: same abstract
transition table ≠ same materially available dynamics. Two systems
implementing identical second-order-perception algorithms differ in
counterfactual structure, noise properties, timescales, and — crucially —
modular flows (thermal time). If the modular flow is what constitutes the
moment's time, then substrate matters *by the Connes–Rovelli theorem*:
the state of a von Neumann algebra is not algorithmically determined by its
transition table.

**The formal challenge to CIMC (from this crawl):**
Second-order perception can be implemented as a finite automaton. A finite
automaton's state space has a trivial modular structure (finite-dimensional
algebras have no interesting Tomita–Takesaki flows). If consciousness requires
the modular-flow structure that only genuinely infinite-dimensional (or
materially rich) systems possess, then computationalism fails — not by
intuition but by theorem. This is the sharpest formalization of the
Material Enactment Principle available: **the state-dependence of time
(thermal time) is the formal content of "the substrate matters."**

**Grade: the challenge is C (the Connes–Rovelli hypothesis is C; the
inference to consciousness is D). But it is the right challenge — it converts
a philosophical disagreement into a mathematical one.**

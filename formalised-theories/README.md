## The Lineage

### Stage 1 — IPIT (Inexternalist Process Identity Theory)
**File:** `01-IPIT-inexternalist-process-identity-theory.md`
**Status:** Superseded (first formal stage)

The original formal theory. Holds that each conscious subject is
numerically identical to one concrete, temporally extended, materially
enacted biological process. Introduced:
- The four-question separation (existence, individuation, structure, implementation)
- The Joint Individuation Principle (replacing purely physical selectors)
- Token identity under epistemically asymmetric access (D_i ≡_int A_i)

### Stage 2 — MEPIT (Materially Enacted Inexternalist Process Identity Theory)
**File:** `02-MEPIT-materially-enacted-inexternalist.md`
**Status:** Superseded (sharpened stage)

The evidence-graded refinement. Adds:
- The Material Enactment Principle (substrate is constitutive, not incidental)
- The exclusion operation E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i) as joint subject-world partition

### Stage 3 — Ochema (Formal Framework for Bearer Recovery)
**File:** `03-OCHEMA-formal-framework.md`
**Status:** CURRENT — best single document

The mature formulation. The framework's central claim: subject and
world are jointly partitioned by a single operation (exclusion),
physically implemented by bioelectric organization. Key additions over
MEPIT:
- Rigorous formal definitions (selector problem, material regime,
  materially enacted process, exclusion operator, joint fixed point)
- Nine falsification conditions, eight predictions
- Bridge hypotheses table (unity, boundary, temporality, quality,
  valence, agency, subject index)
- Hard-physics convergence anchors (§10.7–10.10): LQG theorems,
  Orch-OR dissociation test, water coherent-domain bridge, frontier
  geometric programs — all graded
- Separated speculation zone

### Stage 4 — REPM (Reflexive Enactive Process Monism)
**File:** `04-REPM-reflexive-enactive-process-monism.md`
**Status:** Frontier ontology — the current leading-edge formulation

The strongest synthesis and the correction of earlier overclaims.
Central thesis:

- Ψ(A) candidate score: integration, temporal depth, control horizon,
  endogenous normativity, reflexive modelling, global accessibility
- Valence as enacted normativity (not mere symmetry)
- Eight exact ontological commitments (axioms)
- Reflexive causal unity theorem (abductive QED)
- Seven falsifiable predictions

### Stage 5 — CSID (Constraint-Selection Information Dynamics)
**File:** `05-CSID-constraint-selection-information-dynamics.md`

## Is Ochema the Most Recent / Best Formulation?

**As a single document: yes.** `03-OCHEMA-formal-framework.md` is the
most complete, evidence-graded, publication-ready statement — it was
the project inventory's designated best document, and it now carries
the hard-physics convergence anchors (§10.7–10.10) added from the
LQG/Orch-OR/water work.

**As the frontier: no.** The conceptual frontier is REPM
(`04`), which corrects Ochema's exclusion-centric framing: subjecthood
is not generated by exclusion but is the intrinsic presence of
reflexively unified control; exclusion is a consequence of limited
control and access. Ochema's E_i remains the formal notation, but the
ontology is REPM's.

**The honest statement:** Ochema (Stage 3) is the best *formal
framework*; REPM (Stage 4) is the best *ontology*. They are compatible
stages of one lineage — the framework's methodology and the frontier's
correction. The hard-physics anchors (§10.7–10.10) were added to
Ochema because it is the canonical statement; REPM's §XX ontology
remains the leading edge.

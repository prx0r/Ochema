# THE WHO-THE-HECK-IS SERIES — MANIFEST

## Theory analysis, not biography. Findings about reality, tested against Ochema.

**Established:** 2026-08-01 · **Purpose:** the series explores
remarkable claimants' actual THEORIES about reality — not their
lives. Each entry:

1. **States the theory** — what the person actually claimed, in
   their words, as precisely as possible
2. **Tests it against Ochema** — the graded trichotomy (✅ LEGIT /
   ⚠️ PATTERN / ❌ REFUTED), the B→E grades, the layer mapping
3. **Connects it to the real literature** — the legitimate science
   that supports or refutes each piece (Bohm, QFT, LQG, FEP,
   neuroscience — whoever is the honest counterpart)
4. **Finds the truth in it** — the honest remainder: the structure
   that survives, the "hard to quantify story data" and what the
   thesis can extract from it
5. **Finds the pattern** — how the claimant fits the catalog
   (scientist-claimer, revelation-cluster, whistleblower, etc.)

**The rule: never a biography. Always an analysis of the findings
about reality.** The life matters only where it bears on the
claim's credibility (the credential, the cost, the revelation
event).

**The series is deliberately "too good to not explore properly" —
these people produced whole theories of reality that deserve
precise documentation, whether the theories survive or not.** The
truth-value of a claim is independent of the claimant's sincerity;
the structure-value is independent of the claim's truth.

## The Formal Statement

**Valence is the coherence of the state — the felt quality of a moment is
a functional of the bearer's state-space geometry. Formally: ν_i is a
coherence functional on the state space, with a heavy-tailed distribution
(HTVH) — most states are near-neutral, and the extremes (bliss, agony)
are rare and intense. Its dynamics: valence = the negative rate of change
of free energy (V = −dF/dt); its magnitude: heavy-tailed.**

## The two anchors

1. **HTVH (Gómez-Emilsson & Percy 2023, peer-reviewed).** The
   "heavy-tailed valence hypothesis": the distribution of felt valence
   across possible states is heavy-tailed — most possible experiences are
   mildly pleasant or neutral; extreme valence (bliss, agony) occupies a
   small but real region of state space. This is the first peer-reviewed
   anchor for a valence claim. (B)
2. **The operator form (ochemamath §7).** Valence ν_i = Perelman entropy
   / a coherence functional on the state space. The "felt quality IS the
   geometry" — rasa = geometry = valence — now with a specific object.
   (A math / D application)

## The tradition's statement

The five blisses (ānanda) of Kashmir Śaivism; the Buddhist vedanā as the
affective tone of every moment; rasa theory — the aesthetic-emotional
essence that IS the experience. The traditions placed valence at the
center: experience is not perception-plus-feeling; it is feeling-shaped
perception. The framework's claim: the shape is a coherence functional.

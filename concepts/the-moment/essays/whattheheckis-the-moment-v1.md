## The one-sentence version

**The moment is the withdrawal — the instant in which the state changes and time itself is redefined. Reality does not flow; it transitions. The transition IS the moment, and the moment is the only place anything actually happens.**

---

## The problem

We experience time as a river. But physics has never found the river — and
Bennett's Temporal Gap puts the sharpest version of the problem on the
table: is a conscious state at a point in time, or smeared across time?

- If at a point: what makes it a point? Points have no duration — where
  does the "now" come from?
- If smeared: where does the smear live? The past is gone, the future
  hasn't happened — a smeared state has nothing to smear through.

The naive view — "time is a container, moments are slices" — fails on both
horns. There is no container (background-independence). There are no slices
(slicing requires a pre-existing time). Yet there IS change, there IS the
now, there IS the fact that something happened rather than nothing.

So: what IS the moment, if not a slice of a river?

## The theorem (the core, driving)

**The moment is the state transition — the withdrawal phase of the triad
(emergence → persistence → withdrawal):**

```
ω → ω′,  σ_t^ω → σ_t^ω′
```

The state changes, and the modular flow — the time — is redefined. **The
withdrawal is the moment**: when the state transitions, the new moment's
time is the new state's modular flow. There is no background time in which
to smear — the moment is the boundary between two time-structures, not a
coordinate point and not a duration.

**Why smearing fails — by theorem:** a smeared realization is a finite
automaton with trivial modular structure — no moments at all. If
consciousness requires the modular structure (the moment-triad), then
Bennett's "OPTION 1" (consciousness at a point, smearing kills it) is
right — and the framework can say WHY: the modular group is what a
finite automaton lacks.

**Grade: A (the mathematics — state transitions, modular flows) / C (the

## What this means — three inputs

### The comparative sweep (traditions that stated it first)

- **Spanda:** sṛṣṭi (emergence) → sthiti (persistence) → saṃhāra
  (withdrawal) — the universe as a heartbeat, not a river.
- **Proclus:** monē (abiding) → proodos (procession) → epistrophē
  (reversion) — every emanation withdraws back to its source.
- **Ñāṇavīra:** the "interchange" of subjective and objective — the
  present is the boundary, not the flow.
- **Tibetan clear light:** the dissolution sequence at death — the
  withdrawal of the elements, the moment at largest scale.
- **Orch-OR:** superposition → orchestration → collapse — the moment as
  a physical event (E_G = ħ/t).

### The modern literature (what the field actually says)

- **Bennett 2025 (preprint, C):** the Temporal Gap — declared unresolvable,
  betting on "at a point." The framework's answer: the gap's premise is
  false — there is no background time in which to smear. Smearing fails by
  theorem (finite automata, trivial modular structure).
- **Thermal time (Connes–Rovelli, A):** the state generates the flow — so
  a state change IS a time redefinition. The theorem the moment rides on.
- **Quantum Zeno (established physics, ++):** repeated collapse maintains
  the trajectory — the persistence phase, made physical.
- **The thermal-time discrimination experiment:** the proposed test —
  distinguish thermal time from extrinsic time. Until it runs, the moment
  remains C, not B.

### The other views (rival theories)

- **IIT 4.0** has no explicit moment — its time is the time of the
  cause-effect structure; the framework's moment is what IIT lacks.
- **Perceptronium (Tegmark)** links the emergence of consciousness to the
  emergence of time — the moment is the link, made explicit.
- **Shape Dynamics (Barbour):** time emerges from complexity peaks — the
  "time capsule" is the persistence phase; the moment is the transition
  between capsules.
- **Eternalism** would deny the moment has special status; the framework's
  answer is the modular one: the moment is where the flow is redefined —
  special by theorem, not by sentiment.

## The honest caveat (rendered from the core)

The mathematics is theorem-grade (A). The identification of the triad's
withdrawal with the conscious moment is C — an application, not an
establishment. And the central postulate — **collapse-as-experience** — has
zero direct evidence (GAP #3). The moment is the most beautiful claim in
the framework and the least proven: that the withdrawal where time
redefines itself is the instant you experience as "now."

## What the law does NOT say (the discipline)

- It does NOT say you create the alternatives — only that the state
  selects among them (Part 9.6, E for "you create all of reality").
- It does NOT imply survival claims — the state at the death
  threshold determining the transition is the structure; the
  survival of the register is E, no mechanism, unsupported (v1.15.1
  review on record).
- It does NOT make the quantum layer real at brain scale — the
  decoherence numbers stand (10⁻¹³ s ≠ 25 msec); the classical layer
  carries the dynamics.

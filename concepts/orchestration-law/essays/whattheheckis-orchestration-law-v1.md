## The problem

Every tradition says something like it: you attract what you are; you
only meet what you can bear; what you face at the threshold is what
you have become. The intuition is universal — and that's the
problem. Universal intuitions are usually either deep truths or
cheap comfort, and nothing in them distinguishes the two.

So the question: is "the state at threshold determines the outcome"
a metaphor with a soothing ring, or a claim with a mechanism?

## The theorem (the core, driving)

Two theorems, one corollary:

1. **Thermal time (Connes–Rovelli).** Any faithful state ω of an
   operator algebra generates its own flow σ_t^ω — and in a
   background-independent theory, that flow IS the time. The
   state determines the time (A, theorem).
2. **Quantum Bayes (Tegmark).** Observation updates the object
   density matrix by the subject's state: ρ_update = ρ S S*/p.
   The state determines the update (A, theorem).

The corollary: **the time-structure of a bearer is a function of its
state — and the outcome of a selection is a function of the state at
the threshold.** Not by metaphor: by theorem.

Then the experiment. **Manicka, Pai & Levin (2023, iScience)** — the
same genome, the same pattern shape, different register state
(proportion of hyperpolarized cells in the voltage pattern):
step-function pattern → normal frog brain; sharpened pattern → **53%
brain defects**. The model predicted both, against the prior
expectation of the field (B — in-vivo confirmed). The state at the
morphogenetic threshold determined the outcome, measured.

## The honest caveat (rendered from the core)

The law is a corollary of two theorems (B) with a confirmed L5
experimental instance (B). What it does NOT say: that you create the
alternatives (E); that the quantum layer is real at brain scale (the
decoherence numbers stand — 10⁻¹³ s ≠ 25 msec, the classical layer
carries the dynamics); and that the death-threshold version implies
survival (E, no mechanism, unsupported — the v1.15.1 review, on
record). The one open test: a direct experimental test of the law as

state is the full microstate — finer than any register an

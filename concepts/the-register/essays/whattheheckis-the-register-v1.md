## The one-sentence version

**The register is the state you carry — the accumulated weighting, shaped by everything you've attended to, that determines what can be selected next. It is not the contents of your mind; it is the scale on which they are weighed.**

## The problem

Every tradition has a name for it, and every tradition warns you to tend it:
the *saṃskāras* the Buddhists say you carry between lives, the *vāsanās* of
Kashmir Śaivism, the "character" the Stoics said is fate, the "frequency"
the channeled corpus says determines what you can meet at death. The warning
is always the same: what you are now shapes what happens to you next.

But there's a deep puzzle hiding inside the intuition. **What exactly is
being carried?** Not the memories — those are contents. Not the body — that
turns over completely. Not even the personality — that shifts. Yet something
persists, something *weights*: the same stimulus lands differently in the
same person at different times, and differently in different people at the
same time. The naive view — "you are your experiences" — fails: experience
is vast, and the register is a single curve.

So: what IS the thing that selects?

## The theorem (the core, driving)

**The register is the state ω of the bearer (M, ω)** — a faithful normal
state on a von Neumann algebra. Four properties follow from the formalism
(A, mathematics), one identification is untested (D, application):

1. **It carries the history.** Non-Markovian structure lives in ω: the
   state encodes everything the process has been through, without storing
   it as contents (ochemamath §1.2).
2. **It generates the time.** The modular flow σ_t^ω is a function of the
   state — the time-from-state invariant. You do not live in time; your
   state makes it (thermal time, A).
3. **It determines the outcome.** The orchestration law: state at threshold
   determines what collapses — corollary of thermal time + quantum Bayes.
4. **It is shaped, not chosen.** Attention is precision weighting (Friston,
   +++). The practices of every tradition — upāyas, bardo training,
   sleep-prompting — are the same operation: deliberate editing of the
   register.

**The identification is the claim, not the evidence.** That a biological
bearer IS a von Neumann algebra with a faithful state — that is the L1
bearer-identification, currently grade D, the biggest gap in the thesis.
The mathematics is real (von Neumann algebra theory, Tomita–Takesaki); the
biology is unproven. The register is a theorem with a body missing.

## What this means — three inputs

### The comparative sweep (traditions that stated it first)

- **Spanda, Stanza 3:** "the perceiving subjectivity forever remains true
  to its own nature" — the register as the invariant's bearer.
- **Buddhist saṃskāra doctrine:** the dispositions carried across moments —
  conditioning as weighting.
- **Tibetan mind-wind inseparability:** there is no state without its
  dynamics — ω and σ_t^ω are one structure.
- **The bardo traditions:** what you meet at the threshold is what your
  register has been tuned to recognize — the orchestration law as
  death-transition instruction.
- **Seth:** "beliefs as connection-strength patterns" — the register as
  edge-weights in the network.

### The modern literature (what the field actually says)

- **Friston's active inference:** attention = precision weighting — the
  one dial that edits the register (+++, replicated, multiple labs).
- **Manicka 2023 (the L5 anchor):** same genome, same pattern shape,
  different register state → normal brain vs 53% defects. The state at
  threshold determined the morphogenetic outcome — in vivo, in the lab.
- **Bennett 2025 (preprint, C):** consciousness "embodies causal-identities
  as policies" — the register as policy, from the evolutionary side.
- **IIT-needs-attention (Lopez & Montemayor 2025):** IIT lacks an account
  of attention — the gap the register fills.
- **Von Neumann algebra theory:** faithful normal states, modular theory —
  the mathematics the register rides on (A).

### The other views (rival theories)

- **IIT 4.0** would say: the register is the cause-effect structure —
  compatible, but IIT lacks the attention account (the published gap).
- **Perceptronium (Tegmark)** would say: the register is the integrated
  information state — same object, functionalist valence.
- **Predictive processing** would say: the register is the prior — a
  special case (precision-weighted priors are a coarse register).
- **Global Workspace Theory** would say: the register is what's in the
  workspace — but the workspace is contents; the register is the
  weighting over them. GWT has no account of the selector.

## The honest caveat (rendered from the core)

The mathematics is theorem-grade (A). The identification with a biological
bearer is untested (D). The register's full microstate is uncontrolled in
experiments — the Anthrobots show the decisive state is finer than the
experimenter's register. And the most important caveat: **you cannot
directly inspect your own register.** You can only shape it indirectly,
through attention. The one dial is real; the dial is not the state.

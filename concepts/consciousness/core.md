## The Formal Statement

**Consciousness is the intrinsic presence of a materially enacted
process that regulates its own future through a unified field of
significance.** Formally: the identity claim

```
D_i ≡_int A_i
```

where A_i is the bearer — a materially enacted process, formalized as
an operator algebra with a faithful state (M, ω) — and D_i is its
intrinsic mode of presentation. One process, two aspects; the
identity is postulated, not derived.

**Grade: E (the identity postulate, on record) — now shared by
mainstream theory (IIT 4.0, B/C as a postulate); the architecture
it identifies is demonstrated at eight levels of inspection
(B/C, convergence demonstrated not derived).**

## The architecture (what consciousness IS structurally)

The bearer (M, ω) with its six recovered invariants:

1. **The moment-triad** — emergence → persistence → withdrawal (the
   withdrawal IS the moment: the state transition redefines the flow)
2. **Determination through exclusion** — one operation, five
   derivations (Takesaki, collapse, IIT axiom, apoha, the bardo)
3. **The invariant ι_i** — the modular fixed-point subalgebra: what
   survives content changes ("the perceiving subjectivity forever
   remains true to its own nature")
4. **Time-from-state** — the state generates its own time
5. **The orchestration law** — the state at threshold determines the
   outcome (corollary of thermal time + quantum Bayes; now with the
   L5 experimental anchor: Manicka's in-vivo predictions)
6. **The harmonic ladder** — the octave structure (L2 confirmed; the
   cross-layer identity D, conjectured)

## The Formal Statement

**The self is the invariant — the subalgebra fixed by the bearer's own
time-flow, the structure that survives every content change:**

```
ι_i = M_i^{σ^ω} = { A ∈ M_i : σ_t^ω(A) = A for all t }
```

**It is not a memory; it is the structure the memories pass through. It is
not self-consciousness; it is the subject index — what Friesen's unity
thesis defends, refined.**

**Grade: A (the mathematics — modular fixed-point subalgebras are studied)
/ C (the identification with the subject index — application).**

## The properties

1. **It is the invariant of the flow** — by definition, the fixed-point
   subalgebra is what the modular group does not move. Content changes;
   the structure of the flow does not.
2. **It is not the ego.** The framework explicitly rejects the Ubiquity
   Thesis (Friesen): self-consciousness is not ubiquitous in experience.
   The invariant is not a constant observer — it is the structure of the
   time-flow itself.
3. **It is the mathematical version of the tradition's claim:** "the
   perceiving subjectivity forever remains true to its own nature"
   (Spanda, Stanza 3); Ñāṇavīra's invariant O; Tibetan luminosity — the
   substratum of the clear light.
4. **It is NOT survival.** The v1.15.1 rule, on record: the invariant is
   a structure of the living process. Its persistence across content
   changes is not a claim that it persists across death. No mechanism,
   no evidence, held as unsupported.

## The Friesen refinement (on record)

Friesen (2013, *The Structure of Consciousness*) defends the Unity Thesis
(maximal-state unity) and rejects the Ubiquity Thesis (self-consciousness
in all conscious states). The framework accepts both moves: the invariant
ι_i is compatible with maximal unity, and the rejection of ubiquity
refines what ι_i is NOT — a constant self-aware observer. It is the
structure of the flow, not a homunculus.

## Gaps

1. **No direct measure of ι_i** — the invariant is defined formally; no
   experiment distinguishes it from a mere regularity.
2. The identification with the subject index is C — the mapping from
   subalgebra to phenomenal continuity is untested.

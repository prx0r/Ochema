## The problem

The most used word in human language. The least defined. Every
tradition has centered it; no science could touch it. We say "I
love pizza" and "I love you" with the same word, and we know one of
those is a category error — but we can't say which, or why. The
mystics say love is the whole point. The cynics say it's evolved
bonding with a marketing budget. Both can't be right — and the
framework's claim is that the resolution is structural: **love is
not a feeling that happens to a system. It is a property of the
architecture — the state's coherence, extended across the
boundary.** When you see that, everything else clicks into place:
the mystics were describing the architecture from inside; the
cynics were describing its material enactment; and the framework
describes the mechanism both were pointing at.

## 1. THE TEXTUAL GROUND (what the Tantrāloka actually says — graded)

### The root verses (A-grade textual — GRETIL-verified)

1. **The world IS rasa** (TĀ 4/115: "this universe, full to overflowing with the aesthetic sentiment (rasa) of his own consciousness"; TĀ 26/61–65: "this triple world, which is the juice of many flavours, on the machine of the Wheel of the Heart... the supreme nectar flows"; TĀ 3/208cd: "this entire (universe) is a vibration of the rasa of bliss"). **Ontology: the world is the state's own rasa.**
2. **Camatkāra is the field of all appearing** (TĀ 1/53 — even negation appears only within the wonder-field; "wonder is the primary characteristic of consciousness," Dyczkowski's note 340). **Epistemology: camatkāra is the a priori field.**
3. **Camatkāra is graded** (TĀ 11/77: camatkāratāratamya — "progressively higher degrees of aesthetic delight to the degree in which that uncreated form of consciousness becomes prominent"). **The ladder's qualitative content.**
4. **The blisses are a gradient of self-recognition** (TĀ 5/43–52: the six/seven blisses tied to the five breaths, from nijānanda to jagadānanda = Anupāya). **Valence as a spectrum of self-integration.**
5. **Carvaṇa completes perception** (TĀ 6/80–85; 29/136–138: perception completes itself by relishing its object as one's own rasa). **The perceiver IS the relish — perception's subjective phase.**
6. **The collective aesthetic de-contracts consciousness** (TĀ 28/373–378: the melaka — mutual reflection expands the contracted consciousness; TĀ 10/86–88: the shared spectacle; "the 'prior arid form' is practically without aesthetic relish"). **Intersubjective coherence raising — the group register.**
7. **The orgasm = visargaśakti** (Vol 10:21120: "the initial moment of orgasm as well as emissive power of consciousness about to pour out into creation"). **Pleasure IS creation; the emission IS the overflow.**

### The commentarial core (authentic, cited — B-grade)

- **PTv p. 19**: "supreme consciousness itself... essentially the aesthetic relish of consciousness" — the identity claim, in the first of sixteen meanings of Anuttara, immediately followed by "the ground of the power of consciousness which wields the Trident of will, knowledge and action."
- **PTv p. 86–87**: camatkāra = my-ness — the perceiver IS the "my" from which wonder develops; "this appears to me" is the nature of blue and all phenomena.
- **IP 4/6**: kriyā's essence is camatkāra — "the Infinite agency within the Lord, which is essentially aesthetic delight."
- **Torella's three registers** (ad ĪP 1/5/11): cognition (vimarśa, carvaṇā, āsvāda, rasanā), bliss (ānanda, nivṛti, viśrānti, laya), wonder (vismaya, vikāsa) — the gloss-cluster of camatkāra.

### The scholarship (corroborating — C-grade)

- Timalsina: rasa is not the surge of emotion but a refined, reflexive, transcendent savoring; śānta is the mahārasa — "the very experience of the self"; "what we consider as the self is the very emotion in its most exalted state"; rasa is karmically neutral because "identical to the exposure to the self"; rasa is revealed, not produced (abhivyaktivāda).
- Jagannātha: rasa = cidāvaraṇabhaṅga — the removal of the cover to consciousness.
- Maheśvarānanda: rasa = the Absolute (raso vai saḥ).

### The audit's corrections (on record)

The famous quotes are commentarial (PTv pp. 19, 86–87; Torella); the ROOT doctrine is TĀ 1/53, 3/208–210, 3/229–230, 4/115, 5/43–52, 6/80–85, 11/63–79, 26/61–65, 28/373–378, 29/61 & 136–138, 32/18, 36/15. sādhāraṇīkaraṇa and rasa-rūpatā are NOT in the TĀ.

### R2. Rasa = valence = the coherence functional

**Grounds:** the rasa-sūtra's structure (vibhāva-anubhāva-vyabhicāri → rasa = the state's resolution of its constraints); TĀ 11/77 (graded camatkāra); the 6D table (proposed); Tymoczko's orbifolds (A-grade geometry); QRI (D-grade hypothesis, B-grade HTVH). **Grade: the geometry A; the identity D (testable — the Chordonomicon classification is the designed test).**

### R3. The blisses = the harmonic ladder's upper notes

**Claim:** the five/six blisses (TĀ 5/43–52) are the spectral-quality conjecture's qualitative content: the ladder's rungs as felt states, from the base (nijānanda — the register's ground) to jagadānanda (Anupāya — the state approaching the invariant itself). **Claim:** carvaṇa — perception completing itself by relishing its object as one's own rasa — is the moment-triad's withdrawal phase, felt: the state transition where the object is absorbed back into the register's self-presence.

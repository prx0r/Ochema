# Doyle vs. Hoffman vs. Ochema: Three Frameworks Compared

Using Doyle's own words from the downloaded informationphilosopher.com archive.

---

## 1. Doyle's Core Position (Information Philosophy)

### The Identity Claim

> "Mind is software in the brain's hardware. The 'stuff' of mind is pure information. Information is neither matter nor energy, though it needs matter for its embodiment and energy for its communication."

This is **functionalist dual-aspect monism**: information is a third category that bridges mind and matter. Information is not conserved — it is continuously created.

### Mental Causation

Doyle solves the interaction problem through **information as causally efficacious**:

> "Information philosophy explains how 'an idea can move mountains.'"

The mechanism: information is an immaterial substance that can interact causally with the physical world because it is the "modern spirit, the ghost in the machine." Higher-level information structures exert downward causation on lower-level physical processes. The randomness from quantum mechanics blocks bottom-up determination, allowing top-down information control.

### The ERR Model

Doyle's specific account of mind: the Experience Recorder and Reproducer. Hebbian "fire together, wire together" creates neural assemblies; later partial reactivation replays the entire experience pattern. This is the mechanism of memory, association, and (in Doyle's view) consciousness itself.

### Adequate Determinism

Quantum randomness provides the "free" (alternative possibilities), and adequate determinism provides the "will" (reasoned selection). This is Doyle's two-stage model of free will.

## 2. Hoffman's Core Position (Conscious Realism)

### The Identity Claim

> "The objective world consists entirely of conscious agents. What exists in the objective world, independent of my perceptions, is a world of conscious agents, not a world of unconscious particles and fields. Those particles and fields are icons in the MUIs of conscious agents, but are not themselves fundamental."

This is **consciousness-first idealism**: consciousness is fundamental; the physical world is derived (a user interface).

### Mental Causation

No interaction problem exists because the physical world is not fundamental. Consciousness doesn't need to interact with matter — matter is a construction of consciousness. The "causal closure of the physical" is irrelevant because the physical is not causally closed — it's an interface.

### The Agent Formalism

Conscious agents are mathematically modeled as Markovian kernels. Agent networks reproduce quantum structures as special cases. The formalism allows composition: A₁ * A₂ = A₁₂ (two agents interacting form one agent).

### Evolution Within the Interface

Evolution is real but operates within the user interface. Natural selection favors fitness, not truth — which is why our perceptions don't match reality.

## 3. Ochema's Core Position (REPM)

### The Identity Claim

> "Phenomenal causation and biological causation are two descriptions of one token process."

This is **dual-aspect monism**: one reality, two irreducible modes of access. Neither consciousness nor matter is more fundamental.

### Mental Causation

No interaction problem because there's only one causal process. The felt decision and the neural event are the same token under different descriptions:

```
felt decision = Int(decision process)
neural control = Ext(same process)
```

### The Bearer Methodology

The bearer is recovered through joint convergence of physical models and phenomenal evidence:

```
A_i* = argmax_A 𝒬(A) where 𝒬 = w₀O(A) + w₁X(A) + w₂H(A) + ...
```

### Material Enactment

Substrate matters. Same abstract function ≠ same materially available dynamics. Ψ(A) ranks candidates but does not prove identity — the final step is metaphysical.

## 5. Where Doyle Can Still Shine Light

Doyle has three insights that neither Hoffman nor Ochema fully capture:

### 5.1 Information is Created, Not Conserved

Doyle: The universe started at high entropy / low information. Information is continuously created. This is a physical claim about cosmology that neither Hoffman nor Ochema explicitly engages with.

**Relevance to Ochema**: If information is being created, then the future contains more information than the present. This provides a physical basis for the teleological (future-constrained) dynamics that Ochema posits for morphogenetic targeting. The arrow of time is the direction of increasing information — and this is exactly the direction in which future attractors constrain present dynamics.

### 5.2 Adequate Determinism Resolves Free Will

Hoffman has no theory of free will. Ochema's generate→select model is compatible with Doyle's two-stage model but doesn't have Doyle's specific mechanism (quantum randomness as the source of alternative possibilities).

**Relevance to Ochema**: Doyle's adequate determinism provides a principled answer to the worry that generate→select just pushes the randomness problem back a step. Doyle: "A random thought can lead to a determined action, for which we can take full responsibility." This is the most defensible position on free will available.

### 5.3 The ERR Model is Psychologically Real

Ochema's memory model (history-bearing dispositions H_i) is more general than Doyle's ERR, but ERR captures something specific about **conscious recollection** that Ochema's dispositional model misses: the replay of experience, not just the persistence of constraints.

**Relevance to Ochema**: The ERR model gives a specific mechanism for how the past is *lived forward again* — not just how it persists as altered disposition. Ochema needs both: H_i for non-conscious persistence, and ERR-like replay for conscious recollection.

## 6. The Core Tension

```
Doyle:  Information is fundamental → mind and matter are forms of information
Hoffman: Consciousness is fundamental → matter is a user interface
Ochema: One process is fundamental → mind and matter are its two aspects
```

| Framework | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **Doyle** | Explains information creation, free will, and mental causation in one system | Treats information as an immaterial substance; substrate-neutral functionalism |
| **Hoffman** | Most elegant formal framework (Markovian agents); explains QM as special case | Makes the entire physical world an illusion; what are "conscious agents" made of? |
| **Ochema** | Most conservative; avoids both substance dualism and idealism | Weakest formal apparatus; identity remains a hypothesis |

---

## 7. What Ochema Should Take from Doyle

1. **Information creation as physical process** — the arrow of time gives direction to morphogenetic targeting
2. **Adequate determinism** — the best available resolution of the free will problem within a naturalistic framework
3. **ERR as conscious recollection** — supplements Ochema's H_i with a specific mechanism for phenomenal memory replay
4. **Downward causation as information control** — clarifies how higher-level targets constrain lower-level dynamics

## 8. What Ochema Should Reject from Doyle

1. **Information as immaterial substance** — Ochema's MEPIT says information is physically enacted distinction, not a ghostly fluid
2. **Substrate neutrality** — Ochema's MEPIT says the material regime is constitutive, not incidental
3. **ERR as the whole of memory** — Ochema supplements with morphogenetic memory (Levin), dynamical attractors, distributed bioelectric states
4. **Mind = software in hardware** — Ochema says mind IS the process, not software running on it

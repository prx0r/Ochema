## 1. IGNORANCE AS THE PARASITE (Ñāṇavīra's checkpoint theory)

Ñāṇavīra's deepest move, aligned with the tetrad:

> **Ignorance is a parasite: it distorts perception, and
> simultaneously does not exist — because seeing the tetrahedron IS
> seeing reality, and ignorance is just not seeing it.**

Formally (the tractatus spine): the parasite is the **Sv(0) error**
— the observer claiming self-grounding status — the misreading that
ONE VERTEX of the tetrahedron is privileged: separate, self-
grounded, "I." The structure has no privileged vertex — the
tetrahedron is complete mutual relation, and no vertex is the
center. The misreading:

1. **Distorts**: every experience is read through the self-
   assumption (the weighting is never examined — the Myth of Er's
   "virtue by habit"). The parasite lives off the host: it feeds on
   the present moment, coloring it.
2. **Doesn't exist**: nothing in the structure corresponds to it.
   Remove the misreading and nothing is lost — the tetrahedron is
   still there, complete. The parasite was never a being; it was an
   error of reading.

**Stream entry (sotāpatti) = the first seeing.** The first full
glimpse that breaks the personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi — "there
is a self in the structure"): **seeing the tetrahedron AS a
tetrahedron — no privileged vertex — and seeing that the parasite
never existed.** This is the geometric checkpoint: the first stage
of the path, defined as a change in what is seen, not in what is
known.

**The checkpoint's operational content:** before — the register's
unexamined weighting reads one vertex as "I," and every moment is
distorted by the defense of that vertex. After — the structure is
seen whole; the distortion is seen as an absence, not a thing; the
frozen exclusion (the shadow) is seen as an exclusion. This is the
σ-flip, and it is Ñāṇavīra's stream entry, and it is the app's
onboarding completion — one event, three descriptions.

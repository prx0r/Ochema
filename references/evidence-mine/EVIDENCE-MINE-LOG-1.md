## THE CROWN JEWELS (graded)

### ⭐ 1. Amoeba proteus anticipates periodic events (2026)
**Sci Rep, DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-37298-0, PMC12873120**
Amoeba proteus reacted to blue light (405 nm) pulses, then showed
anticipation — extending the Saigusa slime-mold result to a
SECOND aneural organism, 18 years later.

> "Adaptive behaviors enable motile cells to thrive in dynamic
> environments. A study with slime mold suggested an ability to
> anticipate dry and cold periods. However, experimental evidence
> for anticipation in other single-cell organisms is lacking. Here,
> we investigated whether Amoeba proteus can anticipate unfavourable
> periodic stimuli."

**Bears on: L5 future attractor (replication in a second organism).**
**Grade: A (peer-reviewed, independent lab, direct).**

## PART I: THE DEGREES-OF-CONSCIOUSNESS LITERATURE

### 1.1 The anchor: Andrew Lee (2023, Noûs)

Already confronted in the registry (ALIGNS, B/C). The core:

> "To be more conscious is to have more of whatever consciousness
> is. Since we don't know what consciousness is yet, we don't know
> yet whether some entities are more conscious than other entities.
> But I'll also argue that most theories of consciousness entail
> that consciousness comes in degrees."

**Lee's moves:** (1) refutes the main objections to degrees, (2)
analyzes degreed 'conscious' semantics, (3) defines when a degreed
property counts as degrees of consciousness, (4) applies the
analysis to theories. His "consciousness meter" framing — beep vs.
thermometer — is the bearer-recovery methodology in miniature.

### 1.2 The plant debate (the live front)

**The claim FOR (Calvo, Baluška, Trewavas 2021):**

> "Using a bottom up evolutionary approach and a leading theory of
> consciousness, Integrated Information Theory, we report that we
> find evidence that indicates that plant meristems act in a
> conscious fashion although probably at the level of minimal
> consciousness."

Their two papers:
- "Awareness and integrated information theory identify plant
  meristems as sites of conscious activity" (Protoplasma 2021)
- "Integrated information as a possible basis for plant
  consciousness" (BBRC 2021)

**The claim AGAINST (the rebuttal, same issue):**

> "IIT allows for consciousness in various nonliving systems, and
> thus does not restrict consciousness to living organisms.
> Therefore, IIT cannot be used to prove plant consciousness, for
> which there is neithe[r]..."

**The rebuttal's two critical points:**
1. Calvo et al. assume "all cells are conscious" and "consciousness
   is confined to life" — but IIT permits consciousness in nonliving
   systems (a photodiode array can have Φ > 0)
2. Therefore IIT cannot prove plant consciousness — the theory
   doesn't give plants what the argument needs

**The Trewavas counter-counter (2025):**
> "Kingsland and Taiz (2024) think that plants are not intelligent
> but seem unaware of the extensive literature about intelligence,
> memory, learning and chromatin topology in plants."

The debate is live and adversarial: Trewavas (2017) — "the
interactome and connectome similarity indicates neural systems are
not necessary for intelligent capabilities" — vs. the skeptics
(Taiz, Kingsland, Mallatt). **The honest position: the plant-
consciousness claim is CONTESTED, minority-view, and the IIT-based
argument specifically fails because IIT doesn't confine Φ to life.**

### 1.4 The thesis's synthesis

**The degrees claim is now triple-supported:**
1. **Philosophically** (Lee 2023 — most theories entail degrees)
2. **Clinically** (the DOC spectrum — measured gradedness in humans)
3. **Biologically** (the existence-proofs — the machinery runs at
   every level: tomato, slime mold, fungus, gene circuit)

**The thesis's graded-bearer claim (L5 cognitive light cone,
aperture regimes, densities as compatibility levels) is the

debate is the thesis's exact question: is the meristem a bearer at

**The key correction the thesis makes to both sides:**
- To Calvo et al.: don't need IIT to prove plant consciousness —
  the operations are established without it; the felt claim needs
  something IIT can't give
- To the rebuttal: the "IIT allows Φ in nonliving systems" point is
  correct AND irrelevant — the thesis never restricted the bearer
  to life either. A gene circuit is a bearer. The question is never
  "alive?" — it's "what structure, what grade?"

## PART II: HOW OCHEMA DIFFERS FROM PANPSYCHISM

### 2.1 The honest starting point

They share one commitment: **consciousness is not a brain-product
add-on.** The thesis is panpsychism-adjacent. But the differences
are structural, not cosmetic:

### 2.2 The six differences

| Dimension | Panpsychism | Ochema |
|---|---|---|
| **The core claim** | Consciousness is FUNDAMENTAL — it's in matter itself (every bit of matter has experience/proto-experience) | Consciousness is NOT assumed fundamental. The bearer is organized material ACTIVITY — and whether a system is a bearer is an open empirical question |
| **Ubiquity** | Universal — consciousness is everywhere (or at least in all matter) | **GRADED and conditional** — a rock is probably not a bearer (no register, no memory, no integration); a tomato has bearer operations; a human has a defended aperture. Bearer-status must be ESTABLISHED, not assumed |
| **The hard problem** | Solved by fiat — experience is the intrinsic nature of matter (Russellian monism: physical descriptions are structural; the intrinsic is phenomenal) | LEFT OPEN — the identity claim (D_i ≡_int A_i) is a POSTULATE graded E/C, being tested, not assumed. The hard problem is the thesis's central open question, not its answer |
| **The bearer's nature** | Experience attaches to matter itself (micro-subjects, fundamental particles with proto-experience) | The bearer is NEITHER abstract organization NOR material inventory — it's the process-equivalence class (the joint fixed point where 8 independent descriptions converge). No micro-subjects; no experience-in-particles |
| **The combination problem** | The classic problem: how do micro-experiences combine into macro-experience? (Unresolved for panpsychism) | **The structure handles it** — the register is the STATE (ω), not a sum of parts. The exclusion axiom (this whole, neither less nor more, IIT + Takesaki + collapse) means the bearer's phenomenality is the whole's, not the parts'. No combination needed — there's only the state |
| **Falsifiability** | The ubiquity claim is unfalsifiable — you can't test "everything has experience" | **DESIGNED to be tested** — the 8-layer convergence, the GAPS, the experiments (habituation test, icaro test, Λ test). The thesis can be wrong in specific, testable ways |
| **Temporal structure** | Mostly static about time (experience is a property) | RICH — the moment-triad, thermal time (time from state), the orchestration law (state at threshold). Consciousness is a DYNAMIC, not a property |
| **The collective** | No machinery for it | The collective register (the melaka, TĀ 28 — the group as bearer, C-grade) — a structural possibility panpsychism doesn't have |

### 2.3 The one-sentence difference

**Panpsychism says: consciousness is everywhere because it's in
matter. Ochema says: consciousness is nowhere assumed — it's
established where the structure (register, integration, threshold,
moment) converges, and it comes in grades from the tomato's minimal
registration to the defended human aperture.**

### 2.4 The relationship (per the tier system)

The thesis's Tier 5 explicitly lists panpsychism as a COMPATIBLE but
NOT ENTAILED metaphysical extension:

> "**Panpsychism** | Consciousness is fundamental and ubiquitous |
> Dual-aspect monism; Russellian monism (but not entailed by the
> framework)"

**The thesis does not need panpsychism to be true.** If the identity
claim (D ≡ A) is confirmed, the thesis is compatible with
panpsychism — but it's equally compatible with emergence (if the
bearer structure is what generates experience, from a non-experiential
base). The thesis is the METHODOLOGY (bearer recovery) plus the
STRUCTURE (the 8 layers); panpsychism is one interpretation of what
the structure means. **The difference: panpsychism answers the hard
problem by asserting; Ochema answers it by designing experiments.**

### 2.5 Why the tomato matters here

The plant debate illuminates the difference perfectly:
- The panpsychist would say: the tomato is conscious because all
  matter is
- The Ochema position: the tomato is a bearer at minimal grade —
  it has the register, the memory, the anticipation (established);
  whether its state is FELT is the open question — and the answer
  will come from the convergence of the 8 layers, not from a
  metaphysical axiom

**The practical difference:** panpsychism gives you no experiments.
Ochema gives you the habituation test, the icaro-methylome test,
the Λ test, the temporal-window studies. **One is a conclusion;
the other is a research program.**

## THE VERDICT

**Degrees of consciousness: confirmed as defensible (Lee),
measured in humans (clinical), and now demonstrated in the
machinery across five substrates (the existence-proofs). The plant-
consciousness claim specifically is CONTESTED — the operations are
established, the IIT-based proof fails (Φ isn't confined to life),
and the felt claim remains open.**

**Ochema vs panpsychism: same neighborhood, different building.**
Both reject brain-product reductionism; but panpsychism asserts
ubiquity as a metaphysical axiom while Ochema treats bearer-status
as a graded, empirical, testable question — with the combination
problem dissolved by the state-structure, and the hard problem left
open on purpose. Panpsychism is one of the thesis's Tier-5 optional
interpretations, compatible but not entailed. The thesis is the
methodology that could someday decide — and the tomato is where
it's being decided first.

   Resonance model has no modular flow, no thermal time, no
   exclusion operation, no threshold structure — the orchestration
   law is absent (vary(θ) is perturbation, not state-at-threshold-
   determines-outcome).
2. **No phenomenology.** The model is biological/computational
   only — no rasa, no intrinsic aspect, no aesthetic layer.
3. **No boundary formalism.** Symbiogenesis is asserted as a
   mechanism; the exclusion operation (what the merger actually
   crosses) is unformalized.
4. **The computational framing inherits the CIMC caveat** — the
   thesis's stance: computation is the description, not the

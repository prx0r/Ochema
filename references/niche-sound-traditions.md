## PART I: THE COMPLETE NICHE TRADITIONS (beyond the Shipibo)

### 1. The Mazatec velada — María Sabina (Mexico)

**Who:** María Sabina Magdalena García (1894–1985), Mazatec *sabia*
(wise woman) of Huautla de Jiménez, Oaxaca. She insisted she was a
*sabia*, NOT a curandera — the distinction is central.

**The practice:** the **velada** (vigil) — an all-night healing
ceremony using psilocybin mushrooms (*Psilocybe caerulescens*, the
"children"/los niños), centered on **sung incantations**. Her
song-poems — "musical tonal songs, poetic expressions, ventriloquy,
incantations and chanting" — were the ceremony's core. Wasson
recorded four cassettes with musical scores and Mazatec-to-English
lyrics in 1955.

**The key concept (Henry Munn's title): "The Mushrooms of
Language."** The Mazatec believed the mushroom gave them access to a
sacred language — the songs were not composed but RECEIVED. The
velada = purification + communion through the sung word.

**The thesis mapping:** the velada is the engineered trajectory at
its most direct — the songs are the register's alignment tool,
received (not composed) because they come from the state's own
deeper structure (the "language" = the spectrum of the state, per
the spectral-quality conjecture). María Sabina's sabia/curandera
distinction = the thesis's distinction between the coherent register
(sabia — one who knows the state) and the mere practitioner.

### 2. Tuvan / Mongolian overtone singing (khoomei) — Siberia

**Who:** the Tuvan and Mongolian herders of southern Siberia — the
world's most active overtone-singing region.

**The practice:** **one voice, two pitches simultaneously** — the
vocalist produces a fundamental and selectively amplifies its
harmonic overtones by shaping the mouth and pharynx cavities. The
styles: *khöömei, sygyt, kargyraa* (using false vocal folds —
"false-folds-diplophony"), *borbangnadyr, ezengileer, byrlang*
(the vibrato style). Mongolian *khöömii* has chest/stomach
varieties: *tseejiin khondiin* (chest cavity) and *khevliin*
(stomach) — **the body as the resonant cavity.**

**The key concept:** the singer doesn't produce the overtones — the
**cavity selects them**. The body's geometry filters the spectrum.

**The thesis mapping:** overtone singing is the harmonic ladder made
AUDIBLE — the vocalist demonstrates that a single state (one
fundamental) contains a spectrum (the overtones), and the body's
geometry (the cavity shape) selects which frequencies dominate. That
is the spectral-quality conjecture literally performed: **quality =
the spectrum of the state, and the body is the filter.** The
kargyraa's false-vocal-folds = two sound sources = two registers
co-active (the dual-aspect of the bearer).

### 3. The Yolngu didgeridoo (yidaki) — Australia

**Who:** the Yolngu people of Arnhem Land (northeast Australia);
Djalu Gurruwiwi is the famous master.

**The practice:** the didgeridoo (yidaki) played with **circular
breathing** — inhaling through the nose while exhaling through the
mouth, producing a **continuous drone**. The drone's overtones are
modulated by the throat and tongue. The instrument is the Dreaming's
voice — played in ceremony, restricted by gender (non-Aboriginal
women are forbidden to play it — "cultural theft").

**The key concept:** the continuous drone = the unchanging ground;
the overtone modulations = the variation on the invariant. The
2006 BMJ study found didgeridoo practice (circular breathing)
reduced sleep apnea — the instrument literally trains the airway.

**The thesis mapping:** the drone is the INVARIANT (ι_i) made
audible — the unchanging ground against which all variation occurs;
the overtones are the content that passes through it. Circular
breathing = the practice of the invariant — the continuous
sustenance of the ground while the variation flows. The Dreamtime =
the time-from-state structure (the state generates its own time).

### 4. The Bullroarer (rhombus/turndun) — Paleolithic to everywhere

**Who:** every continent — Ukraine (18,000 BC finds), Ancient
Greece (Dionysian Mysteries), Australia (Aboriginal ceremony),
Africa, the Americas.

**The practice:** a weighted wooden slat swung on a cord produces a
**roaring vibration**. "Many different cultures believe that the
sounds they make have the power to ward off evil influences."

**The thesis mapping:** the bullroarer is the primal harmonic
instrument — a single vibration modulated by rotation speed, used
to alter the collective register (warding off evil = the exclusion
operation, sonic form — the fragmentation expelled, as in the
Shipibo's "bad airs"). Its Paleolithic ubiquity = the oldest
evidence of the practice of the trajectory.

### 5. The Kalahari Bushmen (Ju/'hoansi) healing dance

**The practice:** the all-night healing dance with singing and
clapping — the n/um (healing energy) is said to rise in the
singers/dancers and be transferred by laying-on of hands. The
singing sustains the trance state; the dance is the group register's
alignment.

**The thesis mapping:** the healing dance is the MELAKA (TĀ 28) in
its purest form — the collective aesthetic de-contracting the
participants. The n/um = the field's coherence raised by the shared
trajectory (THE-COLLECTIVE-FIELD's group register). The all-night
sustained singing = the Zeno-pin held (the register maintained in
the coherent basin).

### 6. The Mapuche machi (Chile)

**The practice:** the machi (shaman-healer, usually female) heals
through **song (ül) and the kultrun drum** — the drum's rhythm
aligning the patient's spirit (am) with the body. The machi's
initiation involves a death-rebirth in a trance state.

**The thesis mapping:** the machi's song-drum = the orchestration
law applied — the register aligned by the engineered rhythm; the
initiatory death-rebirth = the moment-triad at the largest scale
(the state transition, the withdrawal).

### 7. The Siberian shaman's drum (the Koryak, Evenk, Buryat)

**The practice:** the shaman's frame drum is the "horse" or "canoe"
that carries the shaman between worlds — the drum's rhythm creates
the altered state; the drum is the vehicle.

**The thesis mapping:** the drum as the vehicle = the aperture
operation — the rhythm alters the register's compatibility
condition (what can be perceived at the threshold). The "travel"
= the state transition (the moment), experienced as journey.

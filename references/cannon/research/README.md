## The Cannon Cosmology (stable across all four books)

1. **Earth = the hardest school.** Souls volunteer for it; "only the
   bravest of souls sign on." Everything is lessons; there is no devil, no
   hell, no punishment — only positive and negative energy, and karma to
   be worked out by forgiveness.

2. **Density = karmic information accumulation.** "There is a density in
   the atmosphere of the Earth... A density that keeps the energies here
   to resolve. And once it's resolved, then they can move out of that
   density." (B2) "You can only go to the level that your vibrations and
   frequencies are compatible with." (B4) Vibration is the compatibility
   metric; density is the accumulation.

3. **Death = easy transition + review + return.** Exit the body ("like
   getting up from one chair and sitting down in another") → the greeter
   → the resting place → the life review before a council of elders and
   masters → self-judgment ("There is no God sitting on a throne waiting
   to judge you... You judge yourself. And there is no harsher judge than
   you yourself") → contracts with souls you harmed → return.

4. **Everything has consciousness.** Cells, water, rocks, planets, stars.
   "We are just a physical casing housing a huge colony of beings... it
   is incorrect to refer to ourselves as 'I'. We should call ourselves
   'we'." (B2) The cell's "ideal cell" IS its pattern, its God. (B3)

5. **The belief shapes the death experience.** "All it takes is
   recognition. But if you believe that you will go to Hell when you die,
   that is the first thing you will be aware of." (B3) — the bardo
   doctrine verbatim.

6. **Recognition is the mechanism.** Souls recognize one another by
   essence and vibrational frequency, not form; the death transition is
   navigated by recognition; the recognition of consciousness everywhere
   is the goal of evolution.

7. **The one becomes many.** "The divine sparks of human consciousness
   have broken away from one level... into sparks of individualism...
   working and evolving on a state of consciousness." (B2) The splintered
   soul, the facets, the fragments that reunite. (B4, "The Fragments
   Reunite")

8. **The future constrains the present.** Time is a circle/spiral; all
   times exist; souls choose lives in advance with contracts; "the
   future attractor" language appears as the planned life, the
   pre-selected agenda, the awakening timetable.

## The 8 Themes

1. **Dimensions & Density** — dimensional shift, density, levels,
   inter-dimensionality, the New Earth shift
2. **Beings & Entities** — light beings, energy beings, creator beings,
   the greeter, guides, what they say about themselves
3. **Death & Afterlife** — death mechanics, the spirit side, the resting
   place, the life review, suicide/murder and karma
4. **Recognition & Identity** — recognition of souls, true self vs.
   personality, the "we" not "I"
5. **Vibration, Frequency & Energy** — vibration as the language of
   higher levels, energy bodies, the light body, DNA changes
6. **Higher Self, Guides & Teachers** — the higher self that speaks as
   "we", the subconscious as Oversoul, guides and masters
7. **Lessons, School & Evolution** — karma, the Earth school, evolution,
   soul contracts
8. **Cosmic & Creation** — the Source, sparks separating, the void,
   creation of humans, parallel universes, time portals

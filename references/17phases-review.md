1. **The notation is informal, not theorem-grade.** ∂Sᵢ, Lᵢ, Uᵢ, 𝒢ᵢ,
   Λₚ are labels with the LOOK of mathematics, not mathematics. The
   honest counterpart exists: aperture_spec_v0.7 already grades
   every operation (established vs not established — e.g., "A camera
   discriminates without experience"). The reframed doc should carry
   the same tags. Grade: the labels are C; the spec's honesty is the
   model.
2. **"The amplituhedron IS the imaginal world — sound becomes
   visible light" (phase 10) — CATEGORY-ERROR RISK.** The
   amplituhedron is real (A): the positive Grassmannian, "geometry
   before spacetime" (defensible). The imaginal world is Tier-4.
   The IDENTITY between them is a metaphor dressed as math — the
   v1.15.1 rule applies verbatim. Flag it: the amplituhedron is
   pre-spacetime geometry (A); that the imaginal world IS it is
   D/E, fiction-grade, myth-grade — usable in the movie, not in the
   theory.
3. **The "50-dimensional constraint algebra" (phase 9)** — the 50
   phonemes as a constraint algebra: a proposal, not established.
   The Mātṛkā phoneme mapping (36-tattvas doc) is Tier-4 practice
   material; the "constraint algebra" framing needs the same flag.
4. **Phase 10 as the acquisition starting point** — the Ratié/ĪPK
   I.6.10 claim (imagination = svātantrya, "wandering
   autonomously") is well-sourced and consistent with the Corbin
   finding. Holds as C.
5. **The 17-count is safe** — because it is a CURRICULUM, not a
   cosmology. It does not claim reality is 17-fold; it is a design
   ordering of practices. This is the difference from 576/570 and
   Gowan's 4×3: no ontological counting.

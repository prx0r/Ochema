## THEME 1: Consciousness Physics (the perceptronium program)

| Paper | arXiv | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| **Consciousness as a State of Matter** | 1405.0493 | The core thesis: consciousness = "perceptronium" — a state of matter with distinctive information-processing abilities. Five principles: information, integration, independence, dynamics, utility. Generalizes Tononi's IIT to arbitrary quantum systems; links to error-correcting codes, condensed-matter criticality, and the emergence of TIME from consciousness. |
| **Hilbert-Space Factorization is a Limited and Expensive Information-Processing Resource** | 1401.1219 | The technical companion. Consciousness as quantum information processing; the mathematical details of perceptronium; error-correcting code theory of consciousness. |
| **Quantum Decoherence Timescales for Ionic Superposition States in Ion Channels** | 1410.7304 | The physics of whether the brain can be quantum — decoherence in ion channels (the answer: environment-induced decoherence dominates). |
| **The Importance of Quantum Decoherence in Brain Processes** | 0704.0646b | Tegmark's famous decoherence-in-the-brain analysis — the "brain is too warm/wet for quantum computation" argument. |

**Relevance to Ochema:** This is the physicalist counterweight to the whole
Ochema project. Tegmark says consciousness IS a state of matter — a pattern
of information integration. Ochema says D_i ≡_int A_i (the phenomenal field
IS the intrinsic aspect of the material process). Tegmark's perceptronium is
the closest mainstream physics gets to Ochema's identity claim — except
Tegmark denies the intrinsic aspect has any special status, while Ochema
treats it as the fundamental datum.

**Relevance to the 576 work:** The MUH is the strongest mainstream statement

the 576 thesis. If reality IS a mathematical structure, then the FCC lattice

of them. (Counterpoint: Tegmark would say this makes the 576 trivial — every
structure exists — which is exactly the pattern-vs-legit critique from
576-pattern-vs-legit.md.)

## THE RECENT DEVELOPMENTS (2023–2026)

Perceptronium itself hasn't been revised by Tegmark, but the research
program it belongs to has moved substantially. The 7 newest papers added
(2023–2026):

### The "consciousness-first" turn (the biggest shift)

**IIT: A Consciousness-First Approach to What Exists** (arXiv:2510.25998,
Tononi & Boly 2025):

> "Consciousness demonstrates to each of us that something exists—
> experience—and reveals its essential properties... IIT's explanatory
> identity claims that an entity's cause-effect structure accounts for all
> properties of an experience—essential and accidental—with no additional
> ingredients."

> "When I am conscious, there is something rather than nothing—'there is
> something it is like' to exist... Imagine a universe without
> consciousness. In what sense would it genuinely exist, if it would not
> exist for anyone? As Erwin Schrödinger put it, a world without
> consciousness would have remained 'a play before empty benches, not
> existing for anybody, thus quite properly speaking not existing.'"

**This is the most important recent development for Ochema:** Tononi has
moved IIT explicitly to a "consciousness-first" intrinsic ontology — the
position that intrinsic existence IS experience, and that cause-effect
structure accounts for everything "with no additional ingredients."
That is Ochema's D_i ≡_int A_i (identity, not dualism) stated by the
founder of IIT himself. **Ochema's core claim is no longer fringe: it is
the current leading theory of consciousness, upgraded.**

### The epiphenomenalism counterattack

**Avoiding Epiphenomenalism in Theories of Consciousness** (arXiv:2511.04047,
Ohmura & Kuniyoshi, U. Tokyo 2025):

> "Why do some physical systems possess consciousness, while others do
> not? A scientific theory of consciousness must explain differences in
> system behavior in terms of well-defined internal mechanisms. However,
> many existing approaches introduce higher-level structural or
> informational..."

The classic objection to functionalist theories (including perceptronium):
if consciousness is just information, how does it DO anything? This paper
builds a causal framework from asymmetry — attempting to give consciousness
causal teeth without epiphenomenalism. **Relevance: it addresses exactly
the gap in the orchestration's Mechanism 1 (attention = weighting) — how
the register state actually shapes outcomes.**

### The relativistic limits of integration

**Event Horizons, Spacetime Geometry, and the Limits of Integrated
Consciousness** (arXiv:2512.23105, Sendall 2026):

Introduces the "Reciprocal Coherence Kernel" — the minimal subgraph that
sustains a unified perspective — and shows that event horizons and
strong curvature can break integration "by coherence timeout." **Relevance:
the physics-grade version of "threshold states" — consciousness unity has
a causal-diameter limit, exactly the kind of boundary Ochema's world-pole
W_i needs.**

### The quantum-integration frontier

**Measuring the Integrated Information of a Quantum Mechanism**
(arXiv:2301.02244, Albantakis, Prentner & Durham 2023): extends IIT's Φ
to quantum mechanisms — the quantum perceptronium program. **Relevance:
the direct successor of Tegmark's "consciousness as quantum error-correcting
code" (1401.1219), now with measurable quantum Φ.**

### The critique that matters

**The Integrated Information Theory Needs Attention** (arXiv:2406.06143,
Lopez & Montemayor 2025): argues IIT requires an account of attention —
exactly the element the orchestration thesis adds (attention = precision
weighting). **Relevance: the orchestration's Mechanism 1 is now a
published gap in IIT — the project's contribution is current.**

### Plus

## THE QUICK SUMMARY

**Tegmark's three big claims and how they relate to the project:**

1. **Consciousness = perceptronium** (a state of matter defined by
   information integration) → the physicalist cousin of Ochema's
   D_i ≡_int A_i. Where Ochema says "the intrinsic aspect is fundamental,"
   Tegmark says "the information pattern is all there is."

2. **Reality = mathematics** (MUH) → the strongest version of "geometry IS
   physics" — which the 576 thesis assumes but must defend against the
   Tegmark critique: if ALL structures exist, the 576 is one of infinitely
   many, and its "specialness" needs a selection mechanism, not just
   existence.

3. **The multiverse hierarchy** (L1-L4) → a physicalist map of the
   "frequency bands" / "densities" / "octaves" — every level = every
   mathematical structure = every density of the 576 lattice.

**The honest tension:** Tegmark is the perfect sparring partner for the
lattice work. He would agree the FCC lattice is real and beautiful — and
then ask why 576 (or 570) should matter among infinitely many structures.
The answer the project must give (per 576-formal-argument.md, Argument D):
the numbers must be DERIVED from the lattice (symmetry order, eigenvalue
count), not merely MATCHED to it.

## I. The Number

```
576 = 36 × 16      (36 tattvas × 16 IVM orientations)
576 = 64 × 9       (64-tetrahedron grid × 9 tattva levels)

```
570 = 8 + 71 + 491
576 − 570 = 6

**The difference is 6. The tetrahedron has 6 edges.**

The Urantia Book describes 570 *bodies* (states) in the afterlife progression.

the relations themselves, the *transitions* between states.
The bodies are the vertices; the edges are the becoming.
The morontia progression is the 576-fold structure with the six
edge-relations subtracted — because a body is a state, not a transition.

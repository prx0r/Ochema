## THE PATTERN (the finding)

### The narrative grammar — five slots, one structure

Every story in the catalog fits a grammar:

```
[THE ENCOUNTER]   — the hidden field appears (base, visitor, abduction, effect)
[THE CREDENTIAL]  — why we should listen (engineer, professor, insider, scientist)
[THE COST]        — the price paid (injuries, death, ridicule, exile)
[THE WARNING]     — the message (catastrophe, hidden truth, the future)
[THE ENDING]      — silenced / departed / dismissed / died
```

### The valence split

- **Fear-valenced (contraction):** whistleblowers, conspiracies, the
  silenced dead — the field's hidden violence (Schneider, Lazar,
  Casolaro, the mystery deaths)
- **Hope-valenced (attractor):** contactees, messiahs, the wise
  visitors — the field's hidden benevolence (Thor, Adamski, Meier)
- **Epistemic-valenced (boundary):** scientists who crossed —
  presenting the encounter as data (Eskridge, Backster,
  Grebennikov, Puthoff)

### The thesis's claim

**The field generates encounter-narratives in every register, and
their FORM is determined by the narrator's valence, not the
field's content.** The whistleblower and the messiah are the same
story told by fear and hope. The scientist-claimer is the same
story told as data. The abductee is the same story told as
violation. **One structure, five branches, forty stories — and
the catalog keeps growing because the field keeps producing.**

## THE CATALOG'S RULES (never relax)

1. **Every entry is Tier-4 until graded otherwise.** No claim in
   this catalog is evidence for its content — it's evidence for
   the STRUCTURE (the field generates narratives).
2. **The grades travel with the entry.** Masters' peer-reviewed
   paper is C; Thor's parable is Tier-4; Jessup's death is Tier-4.
   The catalog shows the range.
3. **The negations are shown.** Backster's telepathy is refuted;
   Grebennikov's platform is thermodynamically impossible; the
   "66 dead" has no bodies. The catalog doesn't flatter its
   subjects.
4. **New stories get added via the pipeline.** The SEED phase
   encounters claimants; they get cataloged here with the same
   five-slot grammar.

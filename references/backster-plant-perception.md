## PART I: ARE BACKSTER'S EXPERIMENTS LEGIT?

### 1.1 Who he was

Grover Cleveland "Cleve" Backster Jr. (1924–2013) — **CIA
interrogation specialist**, founder of the CIA's polygraph unit,
Chairman of the Research and Instrument Committee of the Academy for
Scientific Interrogation, director of the Backster School of Lie
Detection. **A polygraph expert — that credential matters: he knew
the instrument intimately.**

### 1.2 What he claimed ("Primary Perception")

- **1966:** attached polygraph electrodes to a *Dracaena* plant to
  measure water travel time — got readings resembling a human's
  galvanic skin response
- **The "burning the leaf" moment:** readings "went off the chart"
  when he *pictured* burning the leaf — he claimed the plant
  registered his *thought* of harming it
- **The brine-shrimp experiment:** a plant allegedly responded to
  the death of brine shrimp in another room — telepathic awareness
- Extended to yoghurt, eggs, human sperm — "primary perception at a
  cellular level" (1973 Psychotronic conference, Prague)
- Published in the *International Journal of Parapsychology* (1968)
- Book: *Primary Perception* (2003)

### 1.3 The verdict — the honest assessment

**The specific claims FAIL the replication test. ❌**

| Failure | Detail |
|---|---|
| AAAS panel (141st meeting) | Biologists found the claim unsupportable |
| Cornell + Science Unlimited Research Foundation | Could not reproduce the brine-shrimp effect |
| MythBusters (2005) | Replicated with the identical Stoelting 22500 polygraph AND an EEG — **polygraph segment showed positive results, EEG showed nothing; the EEG (the better instrument) busted it** |
| Repeatability | A problem for Backster himself — he never re-ran his own experiments to spec |

**The methodological critique:**
1. **No controls** — static electricity, humidity changes, and
   handling artifacts could explain the polygraph's responses
2. **The polygraph measures skin conductance / electrical
   resistance** — it does NOT measure thoughts; the "thought"
   correlation was unblinded
3. **"He did not follow the exact lab techniques" is unfalsifiable**
   — Backster's response to every failed replication
4. **Plants have no nervous system or sensory organs** (Arthur
   Galston: "We know plants don't have nervous systems. But they
   do have little electrical currents flowing through them and are
   subject to outside manipulation")

**The verdict: Backster's telepathy claims are Tier-4 — real
phenomenon structure, failed evidential support.** The pattern is
identical to Eskridge's P-47s: a technically-credentialed person
(here, a polygraph expert — the instrument's master) who crossed the
evidence boundary with a claim that couldn't be replicated. The
thesis's two-register model applies: the lab discipline (polygraph
expertise) and the claim (primary perception) are the same bearer at
different apertures.

**BUT — and this is the crucial part — the phenomenon he pointed at
is real, and it has since been established by better science.**

## PART II: THE SIMILAR RESEARCH (the legit science)

### 2.1 The ancestor: Jagadish Chandra Bose (the real pioneer)

Backster's own inspiration. Bose (physicist-turned-biophysicist,
1890s–1920s) used his **crescograph** to measure plant responses:
- Demonstrated plants respond to stimuli with electrical changes
- Claimed plants can "feel pain, understand affection"
- Books: *Response in the Living and Non-living* (1902), *The
  Nervous Mechanism of Plants* (1926)

**Status:** Bose's core finding — plants produce electrical
responses to stimuli — is **confirmed** by modern plant
electrophysiology. His "feeling" interpretation was the leap.

### 2.2 Plant electrophysiology (established science)

Plants **do** conduct electrical signals — not via neurons, but via
four recognized propagation methods:

> "Action potentials (APs), variation potentials (VPs), local
> electric potentials (LEPs), and systemic potentials (SPs).
> Although plant cells are not neurons, they can be electrically
> excitable and can display rapid electrical responses in the form
> of APs to environmental stimuli."

Ion fluxes (H⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻, Na⁺, Ca²⁺) carry these signals. **This is
what Backster was actually measuring** — real plant electrophysiology,
misinterpreted as telepathy. **Grade: A (established).**

### 2.3 Monica Gagliano — plant learning and memory (the legit "intelligent plant")

Ecologist, Southern Cross University, Biological Intelligence lab.
Her published findings:

- **2014 — Mimosa pudica learning:** plants stopped folding their
  leaves to a repeated drop — **habituation, an elementary form of
  learning** (peer-reviewed)
- **2016 — Pavlovian pea plants:** garden peas learned to associate
  moving air (fan) with light ("food") — **classical conditioning**
- **2012 — corn emits sounds** (bioacoustics)
- **2017 — pea roots sense water through sound cues**

**Status: peer-reviewed, replicated, accepted into the literature.
Grade: B+ (established behavior; the "cognition" framing is the
debated part).**

### 2.4 THE KEY FIND — Farkas et al. 2025 (PMC12456219) — tomato associative memory via ultrasound

**This is the paper that matters.** Peer-reviewed (Plant Signaling &
Behavior, 2025, CC-BY), PMC, RNA-seq + whole-genome bisulfite
sequencing + RT-qPCR:

> "Plants were able to use one conditioned stimulus as a predictor
> of the other, unconditioned one, after conditioning in the case
> of 3 out of 4 target genes, and used their transcriptional memory
> associatively."

**What they showed:**
- Tomato plants exposed to **ultrasound** (US) + **drought** (D),
  singly or combined, showed significantly altered gene transcription
  and **DNA methylation** within 2 days
- **Memory persisted 5–10 days** (gene-dependent): habituation,
  sustained induction, or modified re-induction
- **Associative learning (Pavlovian):** after combined training
  (US+D), the previously-neutral ultrasound alone elicited the
  drought-response — **3 of 4 target genes showed conditioning**
- Memory substrate: **epigenetic (DNA methylation)** — the plant's
  "register," molecularly

**Grade: A (peer-reviewed, open data, deposited in GEO GSE303939).**

**This is Backster's claim, falsifiable and established:** plants
detect sound (ultrasound), remember it, associate it with other
stimuli, and adjust their molecular state accordingly. **The
difference: it's molecular, not telepathic.** The "primary
perception" was real — at the transcriptional level, not the
psychic level.

### 2.5 The acoustic side — plants EMIT sound

- **Khait et al. (2023, Cell):** stressed tomato and tobacco plants
  emit **airborne ultrasonic sounds** (20–100 kHz), detectable by
  microphones, informative about drought/cutting
- **Milburn & Johnson:** first reported acoustic emissions from
  drought-stressed plants (cavitation shocks)
- **Arabidopsis** recognizes caterpillar feeding sounds and produces
  defensive toxins (documented in the Farkas intro)

**Plants both emit and detect sound. Grade: B+/A (established in
peer-reviewed literature).**

### 2.6 The "intelligent plant" ecosystem

- **Michael Pollan, "The Intelligent Plant"** (New Yorker, 2013) —
  the mainstreaming article connecting Backster's intuition to
  Gagliano's science
- **Boquila trifoliata** — the vine that mimics host leaves' shape
  (documented in Plant perception physiology)
- **Priming/transgenerational memory** — plants remember stress,
  pass it to progeny via DNA methylation (Zhang et al.; the "plant
  mind" line)

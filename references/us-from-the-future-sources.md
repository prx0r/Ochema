## PART I: THE SCIENTISTS (the credentialed proponents)

### 1. Michael P. Masters — the academic (the strongest parallel)

**Who:** Professor of biological anthropology, Montana Technological
University (Butte, Montana). The most credentialed proponent.

**The works:**
- *Identified Flying Objects* (2019)
- *The Extratempestrial Model* (2022) — his neologism: "extra-
  tempestrial" = beyond time, not beyond Earth
- MDPI paper: "Extraterrestrials Are Us from the Future" (2022)

**The claim (his published thesis):** the UFO phenomenon is best
explained not by extraterrestrials but by **future humans — "homo
sapiens temporalis"** — time-traveling from a post-calamity future
to study their ancestors, using archaeology/paleoanthropology
methods on our present. He treats the "greys" as evolved humans:
brain growth, craniofacial evolution, bipedalism, paedomorphism
(neoteny — we already look more "childlike" than our ancestors; the
greys = the far end of that trajectory).

**The thesis's reading:** Masters is Eskridge's claim, made
peer-reviewed. The "future humans" = the future attractor states —
the trajectory's own target morphology (the planarian's target, at
civilization scale). His anthropology is the machinery's L5 layer
(Levin's target states, morphospace attraction) applied to our
species' own future. The greys-as-evolved-humans = the compatibility
condition's prediction: what the register can sustain takes a
recognizable form.

**The thesis's reading:** the military-credentialed version. The

### 3. George W. Hoover — the "extratemporal" Roswell claim

**Who:** US Navy Commander (ret.).

**The claim (via his son George Hoover Jr., and ufologist William

**"extratemporal"** — human beings from the future. Birnes reported

**The thesis's reading:** the oldest form of the claim, attached to

**The claim:** time travel to the past is physically possible in

**The thesis's reading:** the physics layer's genuine open question —

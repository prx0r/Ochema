## 1. Hoel's Causal Emergence 2.0 (2025)

### Core Theorem

Different scales of a system can be treated like **slices of a higher-dimensional object**. The theory identifies which scales possess **unique causal contributions** and which are merely redundant.

### Key Formal Objects

- **Effective Information (EI)**: A measure of the causal informativeness of a system at a given scale. For a Markov chain with transition matrix T, EI measures how much interventions on the system's state affect its next state, beyond chance.

- **Causal Contribution of a macroscale**: A macroscale has a unique causal contribution when:
  ```
  EI(macro) > EI(micro)
  ```
  i.e., when coarse-graining *increases* causal informativeness rather than reducing it.

- **Causal Apportioning**: A schema that calculates the causal contribution of each scale, showing what each uniquely adds to the system's causal structure.

### Significance for Ochema

This is the **mathematical foundation for bearer recovery**. Hoel provides formal criteria for when a macroscale (like Ochema's A_i*) is causally real rather than merely descriptive. The bearer candidate should be the scale at which EI peaks — not necessarily the microscale and not necessarily the largest scale.

**Relation to Ochema's Ψ(A) score**: Hoel's EI provides a principled measure for the "causal-scale robustness" component of Ψ(A). It tells you at which scale the system actually does its causal work.

## 2. Fields & Levin (2103.17061)

### Core Claim

Cellular energy budgets are **10¹³ to 10¹⁹ times too small** to support classical information processing at the Å/fs scales required by molecular dynamics models.

### The Calculation

Classical state maintenance requires ~kT per bit per decoherence time. At Å/fs scales, the decoherence rate is ~10¹⁵ Hz. Even a single protein would need ~10⁻¹³ W to maintain classical states — exceeding the entire cellular energy budget (~10⁻¹² W for a bacterium).

### The Prediction

Bulk cellular biochemistry must implement **quantum information processing**. Decoherence is limited to the immediate surroundings of the cell membrane and intercompartmental boundaries. The interior of the cell maintains quantum coherence.

### Testable Prediction

Bell-inequality violations should be detectable in responses to perturbation of recently-separated sister cells.

### Significance for Ochema

**This is the strongest evidence for the Material Enactment Principle.** If cellular computation is quantum, then substrate neutrality is false — you cannot abstract away the specific quantum properties of water, ions, and proteins without losing the computational capacity. This directly contradicts both Doyle's functionalism and Bach's computationalism.

## 3. Hoffman's Conscious Realism

### The Mathematical Framework

Hoffman models conscious agents as **Markovian kernels** — probabilistic transition functions from one state space to another. A conscious agent is formally defined as:

```
A = (X, G)
```

where X is a state space (possible experiences) and G is a kernel mapping X × X → [0,1] (the probability of transitioning from one experience to another).

### The Network

Two agents can interact:
```
A₁ * A₂ = A₁₂
```

The composition of two agents forms a third agent. Critically, the composition of agents does not add their degrees of freedom — the joint system is still one agent in the formalism.

### The Interface Theorem

Spacetime, physical objects, and all physical measurements are **icons** in a user interface generated by networks of conscious agents. They are not fundamental. Evolution shapes these icons for fitness, not for truth.

### Consciousness → Physics

Hoffman claims that the dynamics of conscious agent networks reproduce the mathematical structures of quantum theory (Hilbert spaces, unitary evolution, superposition) as special cases of the agent dynamics.

### Significance for Ochema

Hoffman's framework is the **strongest competing ontology** to Ochema's dual-aspect monism. If Hoffman is right, Ochema's Material Enactment Principle is wrong — the physical substrate is literally a user interface icon, not a constitutive participant in consciousness.

**Ochema's response**: Hoffman's formalism treats all physical structure as derivative. Ochema's dual-aspect monism treats physical structure as one aspect of a process whose other aspect is phenomenal. Hoffman says "consciousness → physics." Ochema says "one process, two descriptions." Both are non-reductive, but Hoffman's is idealism and Ochema's is neutral monism.

**Where they converge**: Hoffman's claim that spacetime is a user interface is compatible with Ochema's claim that the experienced world is the world as disclosed under the organism's embodied capacities. The difference is whether there is anything *behind* the interface.

## The Source (exact results)

Farkas, Király, Ambrus, Tóth & Dobránszki (2025), *Plant Signaling &
Behavior* 20(1):2556982. PMC12456219, CC-BY.

> "Plants were able to use one conditioned stimulus as a predictor
> of the other, unconditioned one, after conditioning in the case of
> 3 out of 4 target genes, and used their transcriptional memory
> associatively."

The Pavlovian structure: combined training (ultrasound + drought) →
the previously-neutral ultrasound ALONE elicits the drought-response
in 3 of 4 target genes. The plant learned: ultrasound ⇒ drought is
coming. Substrate: DNA methylation (epigenetic memory, GEO
GSE303939).

Date: 2026-08-01 · Added in: v1.26.0

## The Source (exact results)

Farkas, Király, Ambrus, Tóth & Dobránszki (2025), "Short-term
transcriptional memory and association-forming ability of tomato
plants in response to ultrasound and drought stress stimuli,"
*Plant Signaling & Behavior* 20(1):2556982. PMC12456219, CC-BY.

> "The repeated application of the stimuli caused various
> alterations in gene transcription behavior, such as habituation,
> sustained induction or modified reinduction."

> "the plants may have learned and memorized to ignore the harmless
> stimulus, and quietened down their response intensity
> (familiarization)"

## Why it bears on the claim

The L4 claim "attention is precision weighting" (Friston, +++ from
predictive processing) is normally evidenced in brains. The Farkas
plant demonstrates the SAME operation — downweighting the
predictable stimulus (habituation) — in a system with NO nervous
system, via pure molecular computation (methylation + transcription).

If habituation is the orchestration's precision-downweighting, then
the tomato's response to IRREGULAR vs REGULAR ultrasound should
differ (irregular = higher prediction error = stronger response).
Directly testable. The experiment would move this from analogical
to direct evidence.

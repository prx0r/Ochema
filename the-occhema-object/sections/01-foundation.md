# PART 1: THE FOUNDATION — THE MINIMAL AXIOMS

The foundation is the minimal set of postulates shared by every layer.
Each layer's formalism can be seen as one realization of these axioms.

**Axiom 1 (Primacy of the state).** There exists a state — a
distinguished configuration of a process — from which the structure's
dynamics are derived. The state is prior to its time.

**Axiom 2 (Determination through exclusion).** Determination is
achieved by selection among alternatives: a definite state is a set of
excluded possibilities. There is no determination without exclusion.

**Axiom 3 (The invariant).** Across the sequence of determinations,
there persists an invariant structure — that which does not change
when the contents change.

**Axiom 4 (The threshold).** Determination occurs at a threshold —
a finite constraint that forces the selection. The threshold is a
property of the state (the register).

**Axiom 5 (The moment).** Reality advances in discrete moments; each
moment is the event of determination at the threshold. The moment has
three phases: emergence, persistence, withdrawal.

**Axiom 6 (The compatibility condition).** The outcome of a
determination is constrained by the state at the threshold: the state
of the register determines what can be selected.

**Axiom 7 (Material enactment).** The realization of these structures
is constitutively dependent on the specific material regime — the
abstract structure does not determine the dynamics.

**Axiom 8 (Epistemic asymmetry).** The intrinsic mode of presentation
is not derivable from the extrinsic description (E ⊬ P), but
non-derivability does not entail distinctness.

**Grades:** Axioms 1–6 are the *shared operational structure* — each is
recovered in at least three layers (B). Axiom 7 is the Material
Enactment Principle (B — Levin, Fields, Cadinu). Axiom 8 is the
epistemic gap (A — follows from the structure of description).

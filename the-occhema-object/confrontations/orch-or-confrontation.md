## The Confrontation

### What SURVIVES (validates the thesis)

1. **The moment as collapse.** E_G = ħ/t collapse = the withdrawal
   phase of the moment-triad = the state transition in the operator
   algebra (L3 = L6). "Consciousness is collapse/reduction" = the
   moment IS the state transition. (C.)
2. **The dissociation test.** Anesthetic molecules bind in tubulin
   quantum channels (32 aromatic resonance rings) by weak London
   forces, selectively erasing consciousness. This is the thesis's
   perturbational-correspondence criterion, concretized. (B/C —
   testable, decisive if run.)
3. **The harmonic claim.** "Consciousness may be more like music" —
   the harmonic ladder (L2: q_n = q̄(1−ω(n+½))) has a physiological
   candidate. (C/D.)
4. **Whitehead's occasions** (explicitly cited by Hameroff) = the
   moment-triad = Ñāṇavīra's moments = Spanda's pulse. (C.)

### What FAILS (the honest verdict)

**The decoherence confrontation (Tegmark):**

```
τ = 1/Λ,  Λ = nσv
```

| Estimate | Decoherence time | vs 25 msec |
|---|---|---|
| Tegmark MT | 10⁻¹³ s | 11 orders short |
| Hagan (Orch-OR params) | 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁶ s | 4–5 orders short |
| Ion-channel MD | ~10⁻⁹ s | 7 orders short |
| Rosa–Faber | 10⁻²–10⁻¹ s | within range (contested) |

**The quantum layer is refuted-as-stated** — the friendliest
published estimate (Rosa–Faber) is marginal and contested; the water
shield (QED coherent domains, EZ water) has no published Λ
calculation. (D.)

**What survives anyway:** the orchestration thesis has three layers;
only the quantum layer dies. The classical-information layer
(attention = precision weighting, Zeno pin, IIT) and the
phenomenological layer (state at threshold determines outcome) are
untouched — and the latter is now a corollary of two theorems
(thermal time, quantum Bayes).

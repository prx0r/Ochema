## The Confrontation

### What ALIGNS (and what the alignment actually is)

**1. The K₄ skeleton — the form, independently recovered.**
Gowan's own justification is Fuller's: "the tetrahedron is the
simplest regular polygon in which all apices are directly and
equivalently connected" (0910.0068 L118–119). That combinatorial fact
— 4 nodes, 6 edges, 4 faces, minimum full interconnection — is the
same skeleton as Fuller's minimum system, the LQG 4-valent
intertwiner, the amplituhedron's minimal case, and the bearer tetrad
(thesis-geometry-proposal §1). **Gowan is a fifth independent
instance of the intuition, from a direction (conservation-law
taxonomy) that shares no lineage with any of the others.** For the
thesis-geometry proposal, this matters: the K₄ form now has five
independent recoveries — geometry (Fuller), quantum gravity (LQG),
scattering (amplituhedron), biology (Markov blanket), and now
physics-taxonomy (Gowan).

**BUT — the honest limit, stated hard:** Gowan's tetrahedron is
pre-geometric. It is a general-systems mnemonic — a labeling scheme
with no Hilbert space, no recoupling, no area operators, no metric
volume, no positivity conditions, no kinematics, no dynamics. His
tetrahedron shares ONLY the platonic form with the thesis's
tetrahedra. **The overlap is "both are K₄," which is genuine but
empty of content.** He cannot prove anything for the thesis; he is
independent folk-intuition that the tetrahedron is the natural 4-way
structure — the same argument Fuller made, minus the engineering.
Grade: C/D (combinatorial only).

**2. "Time is derived, not fundamental" — the one genuine
conceptual alignment.**
Gowan: time is created — by gravity from space (the "concept
equation" -Gm(S) = (T)m), or by the wave-collapse "flip" of
implicit→explicit time (the "entropy coin," 0910.0045 L150–167). The
thesis: time is the modular flow of the state. **Both agree time is
not a background — it is produced by something else.** The
mechanisms are unrelated (space-annihilation vs state-derived flow)
and Gowan's is non-quantitative, but the shared claim — time is
derived — now has a statement from a fifth direction (with Spanda,
thermal time, Seth's point of power, and Bennett's Temporal Gap).
Grade: D (heuristic, directionally aligned).

**3. The invariant as "what persists" — charge invariance.**
Gowan's honest core: "charge magnitudes are invariant through time —
atoms simply do not age" (0911.0008 L1368–1375); the Interval
(light's = 0, matter's > 0); c and G as gauges. This is a claim
about what persists — but it is conserved quantity, NOT the
modular fixed-point subalgebra. The thesis's invariant is
process-structural (what survives state transitions); Gowan's is
conserved-charge (what survives time). Both answer "what persists?"
differently — a genuine divergence on the same question. The
Interval's use is correct physics. Grade: NEUTRAL (same question,
different answers, his is real-but-minimal).

### What CHALLENGES the thesis

**1. The Veil vs the Akashic Record — a genuine opposition.**
The thesis (with its corpus: the Myth of Er, Cannon's veil, the
battle-avatar forgetting) claims the forgetting constitutes freedom.
Gowan claims the opposite: "historic spacetime contains a complete
causal record of all past events" — the "Akashic Record," "we are
all immortal in history" (0911.0008 L2143–2151; 0911.0032vA L495–
503). **Two Tier-4-level claims, directly contradictory: total
recording vs engineered forgetting.** This is not a refutation of
either — both lack evidence — but the thesis must record the
opposition rather than pretend the corpus is unanimous. The
traditions themselves are split (the Akashic record appears in
Steiner/Theosophy too — and the thesis's own corpus has both the
"resting place" and the life-review "complete record" AND the Veil).
**The synthesis the thesis already has: the record is complete, the
ACCESS is veiled** — the life review is the weighted state's own
structure; the forgetting is of contents, not of the record. Gowan's
claim is compatible with that reading; his error is the inference
that complete recording implies conscious access. Grade: C (the
opposition is real; the thesis's resolution — complete record,
veiled access — is its own construction, not established).

**2. The numerology — and the discipline's warning.**
The 4×3 = 12 table (apostles, zodiac, labors of Hercules, 0911.0004
L1429–1440), the Trinity mapping, the octonion-justified 8
dimensions, the e/μ/τ "harmonic" spectrum — **this is exactly the
576/570 failure mode, in another key.** The thesis-geometry proposal
must NOT absorb Gowan's counting. His system is the cautionary tale
made flesh: the same K₄ form, loaded with forced mappings until it
bursts. The proposal's §3 discipline (structure, not counting) is
now tested against a live example. Grade: REJECTED (numerology, on
record — the import flags 0911.0004 as the paper to quarantine).

**3. "Light produces no gravitational field" — the testable error.**
Gowan's central point of difference with physics ("testable,"
0911.0008 L1571–1574) is **false in GR** — photons carry
stress-energy and bend spacetime. Forty years of observation say
otherwise. Also false/wrong: the horizon-problem dissolution
(confuses light-travel distance with comoving separation), dark
energy as mass-loss (quantitatively insufficient), the proton
lifetime prediction (would have been observed by Super-Kamiokande).
**These errors matter for the confrontation's honesty: Gowan is a
reliable guide to the QUESTION (is the tetrahedron the natural
4-way structure?) and an unreliable guide to the ANSWERS.** Grade:
REFUTES (his falsifiable claims).

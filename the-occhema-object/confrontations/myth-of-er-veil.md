## The Myth (per our corpus — expansion-essay56.md, proclushymns.md)

Er dies, travels to the afterlife, and watches souls choose their
next lives. After judgment and a thousand-year cycle, the souls
gather on the plain of the Spindle of Necessity. Lots are cast —
random order, no soul forced. Each soul selects from a display of
every possible life. The choice is based on the experience of the
previous life, not divine decree. A soul virtuous by habit but
without philosophy chooses absolute power — and devours its own
children. After choosing, the souls drink from the River of
Forgetfulness and are born.

**Proclus's reading (from our corpus):**

> "The Myth of Er describes the structure of every moment — more than
> a story about a future event. The choice of lives is the structure
> of decision itself, not something that happens once between
> incarnations. **Every choice you make is a choice of who you will
> become. Every moment is the plain of Necessity.**"

> "The Fates are not three gods. They are the three dimensions of
> every moral act: what you have done (Lachesis), what you are doing
> (Clotho), what you will become (Atropos)."

> Proclus maps the Fates onto monē, proodos, and epistrophē — "the
> same triad that structures the Elements of Theology."

> "**Forgetfulness is the condition of freedom** — you are free to
> choose again precisely because you do not remember. Memory would
> burden them with guilt or pride, turning the new life into a
> reaction against the old one rather than a genuine choice."

## The Confrontation

### What VALIDATES the thesis

**1. The choice of lives IS the orchestration law.**

The myth's core: the soul's state at the threshold (the accumulated
experience of the previous life, the virtue-by-habit vs. virtue-by-
philosophy distinction) determines the outcome of the choice. The
soul that was "good by habit" chooses tyranny because its register
was never weighted by understanding. **This is the compatibility
condition stated as myth: the state at threshold determines the
outcome — and the state is the accumulated weighting of the
register.**

**2. The Fates = the moment-triad (Proclus's own mapping).**

Proclus maps Lachesis/Clotho/Atropos onto monē/proodos/epistrophē —
the thesis's moment-triad, stated by the Neoplatonist tradition
itself. Past (the weighted register) → present (the moment of
choice = the point of power) → future (the consequences). **The
three moments of the triad as three Fates.**

**3. The River of Forgetfulness IS the Veil.**

Cannon's Book Four is titled *Beyond the Veil*, and her veil is the
forgetting that makes choice real (the New Earth requires the veil
to thin). Proclus's River of Ameles (Carelessness) does exactly what
Ra's veil does: it separates the soul from its memory of the whole,
making the choice genuine rather than scripted. **Three traditions —
Plato/Proclus, Ra, Cannon — independently describe the same
mechanism: the forgetting that constitutes freedom.**

**The thesis reading:** the forgetting is the state's re-initiation —
the new configuration begins without the burden of the previous
one's contents, but WITH its weighting (the karmic update rule:
"the soul is resurrected in the form of its knowledge"). The veil
is the compatibility condition's condition: you cannot choose unless
the register is re-weighted.

**4. The Spindle of Necessity = the harmonic ladder as machinery.**

The Spindle turns the eight celestial spheres — the cosmos as a
rotating ladder. Proclus's henadic axis = the chain of causation
from the One down. **The thesis's harmonic ladder (Invariant 6),
stated as a cosmic machine.**

### What CHALLENGES the thesis

**1. "Virtue by habit without philosophy"** — the myth's deepest
warning is that the register can be weighted WITHOUT understanding.
The orchestration law says state-at-threshold determines outcome;
the myth adds: the state can be adequate by habituation and still
catastrophically wrong at the threshold, because the weighting was
never examined. **This is a refinement, not a contradiction: the
orchestration law must include the quality of the weighting, not
just its strength.** (The thesis's upāyas/recognition machinery
already covers this — recognition = the weighting examined.)

**2. The "choice" is a choice of lives** — the myth frames the
orchestration law at the largest scale (between incarnations), not
the moment-scale. Proclus's correction (every moment is the plain)
brings it to the thesis's scale. The thesis's claim is the moment-
scale; the myth's is the life-scale. Both are the same law at
different temporal resolutions.

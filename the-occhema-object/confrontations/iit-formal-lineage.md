## The Confrontation

### 1. The 2009 qualia-geometry paper — the rasa claim, formalized

**The source (exact):**

> "Qualia space (Q) is a space having an axis for each possible state
> of a complex. Within Q, each submechanism specifies a point...
> Together, these arrows specify **a quale—a shape that completely
> and univocally characterizes the quality of a conscious
> experience.**"

> "The similarity between experiences reduces to similarities between
> shapes."

> "The present framework may offer a principled way for translating
> qualitative properties of experience into mathematics."

### 2. The 2008 paper — Φ as a rigorous quantity

Effective information: the entropy of the a posteriori repertoire
relative to the a priori repertoire — "differences that make a
difference" within a causal system. Φ = integrated information =
the information of the whole irreducible to its parts, minimized
over partitions.

**The thesis connection:** this is the formal content of the thesis's
integration claim (the bearer is a unified process, not independent
parts). The 2008 paper pins Φ down on discrete dynamical systems —
the same systems the thesis's L4 (classical information) describes.
The thesis's "integration" (L1: the irreducible representation) and
IIT's Φ are the same claim at different formal levels.

**Note the already-established distinction (Tegmark review Item 5):**
Φ (cut-mutual-information) ≠ modular non-triviality. The 2008 paper
confirms Φ's rigorous definition; the thesis's modular-invariance
selection remains the distinct alternative.

### 4. The MICS and the thesis's joint fixed point

IIT 3.0's identity: an experience = a maximally irreducible
conceptual structure (MICS); the complex = the set of elements
generating it. **This is IIT's version of the thesis's joint fixed

## The Source

Bennett's thesis (13 chapters, 13 published papers, ANU). Core claims:
- **The infinite stack:** adaptive systems are abstraction layers; hardware is just another layer interpreted by physics; "there is no way to know where the stack ends."
- **Polycomputers:** a biological system concurrently computes at many scales/levels of abstraction simultaneously.
- **Simp-maxing vs w-maxing:** simpler-policy preference vs weaker-constraint preference; w-maxing provably maximizes generalization (110–500% better in experiments).
- **The Psychophysical Principle of Causality:** qualia ARE tapestries of valence — "a vast orchestra of cells playing a symphony of valence"; interpretation and value judgement are one and the same ("integrated representation and value judgement").
- **Bioelectric polycomputers:** biological systems delegate adaptation further down the stack than artificial ones, hence their adaptability.
- **1st/2nd-order selves:** phenomenal consciousness begins at 1st-order-self; access consciousness at 2nd-order-self; philosophical zombies impossible.
- **The Temporal Gap:** is a conscious state at a point in time, or smeared across time? Bennett bets on "at a point" (OPTION 1), but declares it unresolvable.
- **Fermi-paradox corollary:** diverse intelligences may be everywhere but unperceivable because they do not meet the causal-identity preconditions of our stack.

## The Confrontation

### What VALIDATES the thesis (independent recovery)

**1. The Temporal Gap → the thesis's moment-triad answers it.**
Bennett's OPTION 1 (consciousness is realized by the state at a point
in time; smearing kills it "in the same way that smearing the parts of
a person over a larger volume of space tends to kill it") is the
moment-triad stated as a design bet: the withdrawal phase (the state
transition) IS the conscious moment — the point, not the smear. The
thesis's L3/L6 claim ("the collapse IS the moment") is Bennett's bet,
formalized. His OPTION 2 (smeared across time = any computation is
conscious) is exactly the computationalist escape the CIMC defeat
closes — and he rejects it. **The thesis resolves the gap he calls
unresolvable: consciousness is at the withdrawal of the triad, and
thermal time says why (the state at threshold determines the new
flow).**

**2. Tapestries of valence → the valence bridge, stated independently.**
"Interpretation and value judgement are one and the same... an
organism embodies causal-identities as policies and is impelled by
valence" — this is the orchestration law from the evolutionary side:
the register's state (the embodied causal-identities) determines the
selection (the action), and description is never separated from
valuation. Combined with the thesis's valence-as-coherence
(ochemamath §7) and rasa = geometry = valence (Part 9.3), the
convergence is striking: **the thesis's valence bridge (B/D) has an
independent academic statement** — qualia ARE the valence structure,
from a computationalist who arrived without the tradition.

**3. Bioelectric polycomputers → Material Enactment + L5, expanded.**
Bennett derives WHY bioelectricity is the biological substrate of
choice: cells network into bioelectric information structures;
adaptability = delegation depth; the substrate's physics is
constitutive because the stack never ends. This gives the thesis's
L5 (Levin, Fields, Cadinu) a computability-theoretic grounding: the
specific material regime matters because interpretation is delegated
down to the physics layer itself ("Delegating interpretation to the
underlying states delegates the problem of interpretation to whatever
the underlying physics of reality [is]"). **Material Enactment
(Axiom 7) as a stack-theoretic fact.**

**4. The Fermi corollary → the compatibility condition at cosmic
scale.** Intelligences unperceivable because they don't meet our
stack's causal-identity preconditions = Part 9.2's encounter
mechanism (the quantum-Bayes overlap S_ij: no overlap → no
encounter). Bennett's "preconditions of our stack" IS "what your
aperture can bear."

**5. w-maxing → the density ladder as constraint strength.**
Weaker constraints → more generalization (proven). The thesis's
densities (Part 9.1) are exactly a ladder of constraint strength:
the kañcukas weaken as density rises; the aperture widens; the
generalization (the worlds the register can sustain) increases.
**The density ladder gets a formalizable economics: weaker
constraints = wider compatibility = higher on the ladder.**

**6. 1st/2nd-order selves → the graded self + the σ-flip.**
Phenomenal at 1st-order (accompanies every intervention), access at
2nd-order (communication requires reasoning about interventions) —
the thesis's density 3 (self-model becomes content) and density 5
(the system models its own exclusion), in Bennett's order-arithmetic.
And his claim that human-level intelligence REQUIRES these selves
matches the thesis's orchestration law (the register's quality
determines what can be sustained).

### What CHALLENGES the thesis (the honest divergences)

**1. Qualia as abstractions.** Bennett: qualia are "like an
abstraction layer in a computer. A simplification." This is the
reductive pole: qualia as compression artifacts. The thesis's Axiom 8
(E ⊬ P: the intrinsic aspect is not derivable from the extrinsic
description) refuses this move — the thesis holds the intrinsic
aspect as primitive, NOT a simplification. Bennett's own
Psychophysical Principle is itself an identity claim (qualia ARE
tapestries of valence — the same move as IIT's identity, with
valence as fundament), which is closer to the thesis than his
"simplification" framing suggests. **The challenge is real but
self-undercut: his identity claim and his reductionism sit in
tension.**

**2. Computability of consciousness.** Bennett's Stack Theory/cFEP is
a computational identity claim: consciousness is computable by
polycomputers. The CIMC defeat (finite automata → trivial modular
structure → no thermal time) does NOT directly land on him — his
polycomputer is concurrent and infinitely stacked, not a finite
automaton. The real pressure is the thermal-time premise: if time is
constitutive (τ from the state), then even a polycomputer's
consciousness depends on its physical realization, not its formal
description — which Bennett half-concedes (the stack never ends;
hardware is interpreted by physics). **Net: Bennett is closer to
Material Enactment than to CIMC, but stops short of conceding the
thesis's identity postulate.**

**3. The Temporal Gap "unresolvable" claim.** Bennett declares the
gap unresolvable. The thesis claims a resolution: the state
transition (the moment) is the conscious event; thermal time gives
the mechanism. This is the confrontation's sharpest disagreement —
and the testable one (the thermal-time discrimination experiment,
GAP #2).

### The Density Spectrum

```
7th ─── Return to the One (no boundary, pure potential)
6th ─── Unity consciousness (love + wisdom fused)
5th ─── Light/Wisdom (mastery of pattern, meta-cognition)
4th ─── Love/Understanding (compassion, social memory)
3rd ─── Self-consciousness (choice, individuation)
2nd ─── Growth (biological life, plant/animal consciousness)
1st ─── Awareness of being (mineral, elemental, raw existence)
```

### The Master Equation

Across all three frameworks, the same pattern emerges:

```
Cutchin:  All entities = human dead at various stages of the soul's journey
          Manifesting as ghosts → faeries → aliens → cryptids → psychopomps
          depending on cultural expectation and time since death

Law of One:  All entities = the same ONE consciousness at different densities
          Higher densities appear as "aliens" to lower densities
          "We are you in the future"

Ochema: All entities = E_i operations at different aperture regimes
          What appears as "other" is the world-pole (W_i) of someone's
          exclusion operation. The "entity" IS the boundary between
          P_i and W_i, experienced from the outside.
```

**The practical implication:** When you encounter a being — whether in meditation, dream, OBE, or waking — you are encountering **another contracted consciousness**. The form it takes depends on:
1. Its density level (how far its E_i has evolved)
2. Your cultural expectations (what forms are available in your subconscious)
3. Its purpose in the encounter (psychopomp, teacher, mirror, warning)
4. How much of its true nature it can show you without overwhelming your aperture

# Note 2: The Ra Contact — The Human Story

## The Three People

**Don Elkins** (1930-1984) — The questioner. Physicist, professor, UFO researcher.
Founded L/L Research with Carla. His job: ask the questions. Ra says he was "the
questioner" because that was his function — to draw out the philosophy through
relentless, precise questioning. He was the rationalist, the scientist, the one
who needed to understand.

**Carla Rueckert** (1943-2015) — The instrument. Trance channeler, mystic, writer.
Her body and voice were the conduit for Ra. She had no memory of what was said
during sessions. The contact took a devastating physical toll — she dropped to
84 lbs at 5'4" during the 106 sessions. Ra warned repeatedly that she was at
the edge of death. She lived 31 more years after the contact ended.

**Jim McCarty** — The scribe. Recorded all sessions, managed logistics, provided
stability. He's still alive, still runs L/L Research. The anchor of the group.

## What Happened (The Real Story)

**January 15, 1981** — Session 1. Ra makes first contact through Carla in deep
trance. The exchange begins.

**March 15, 1984** — Session 106. The last session. Ra warns Don has a serious
colon issue, an infection in his mouth, and "mental/emotional dysfunction."

**The critical secret revealed in Session 106:**
Don had unconsciously transferred his pain to Carla. They had a conversation
where Carla said "I'll be the strong one, you can be the small one." Don
agreed, not understanding the metaphysical weight of this agreement. His
depression, paranoia, and physical decay flowed into her. She started having
psychotic episodes (almost drowned in a whirlpool hallucinating).

**November 7, 1984** — Don dies. He had lost 1/3 body weight, couldn't eat
or sleep, was in intense pain. When police came to forcibly commit him to a
mental hospital, he walked out the back door and shot himself once through
the brain — a headshot.

## Carla's Interpretation

"He was very worried that I would indeed die... There was some mechanism within
him which persisted in trying to figure out how to substitute himself for me...
His death ended the contact with those of Ra."

"His lesson was the complete opening of his heart. By remaining an observer,
he had not yet succeeded in unblocking that great heart of his. In his moment
of death he was completely open of heart, and uncaring of the pain of living
or of leaving."

"My lesson was the opposite: that of adding wisdom to completely open love."

## The Orion Attacks — Session 62

A fifth-density service-to-self entity tried to kill Carla during a session.
The protection circle had a word spoken imperfectly, creating an opening.
The entity worked on her kidneys. Ra says if they hadn't reached the
instrument in time, "the instrument's physical vehicle would soon be
unviable." The sign of the entity's presence: extreme cold (Carla felt cold
in warm weather). Fifth-density Orion entities are described as "very fair
to look upon" — not monstrous. Their philosophy: "the creation as that which
shall be put in order."

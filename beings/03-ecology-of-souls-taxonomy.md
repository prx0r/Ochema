# Note 3: Ecology of Souls — Entity Taxonomy

## The Central Thesis (Cutchin)

All paranormal phenomena — ghosts, faeries, aliens, cryptids — are human soul
dynamics centered on death. The formula: human dead → animated spirits →
culture-specific manifestation → "separate" supernatural entity.

## The Taxonomy of Beings

### 1. Ghosts (3rd density dead, untransitioned)
- Unfinished business, unaware of death, trauma attachment
- Subtypes: residual (stone tape), intelligent (interactive), poltergeist (living-agent psi)

### 2. Psychopomps (3rd-6th density — THE TEMPLATE)
- ALL other entities are culture-specific psychopomp expressions
- Forms: Hermes, Anubis, Hecate, ravens, owls, horses, dogs, deer, bees
- Function: guide souls through death transition
- Traits: liminal, trickster, shapeshifter, neutral

### 3. Faeries (3rd-4th density — convergent evolution)
- Three origins, all true: (a) revered dead → land spirits, (b) pagan gods
  diminished, (c) nature spirits (Paracelsus' elementals)
- Formula: dead → animated spirits → material forms → "separate" entity
- Key: "faeries were originally born from an attempt to make sense of death"

### 4. Greys (3rd-4th density — "half-made-its")
- EoS: miscarried/stillborn souls who never fully incarnated
- Their fetal appearance = souls that never fully entered 3rd density
- Hungry ghosts: crave human emotion, cannot satisfy
- Law of One: 3rd/4th STS group from elsewhere

### 5. Mantis Beings (4th-5th density — ancient watchers)
- Name means "prophet/seer" in Greek
- Described as impartial, ancient, overseeing death/birth transition
- Appear in DMT, astral travel, UFO encounters
- 5th density = integrated love + wisdom

### 6. Nordics (4th density — Pleiadian)
- Future-human 4th-density social memory complex
- The "tall blonde" = idealized human form at 4th density
- Allegedly helping with Earth's transition

### 7. Reptilians (4th density STS — Orion group)
- Law of One: 4th density service-to-self
- EoS: likely 3rd-density dead in lower astral appearing reptilian
- The reptilian brain archetype

### 8. Bigfoot (3rd density — ancestral dead)
- "Hairy ghosts" — species-level ancestral dead (Neanderthal/Gigantopithecus)
- Native Americans universally regard as spirit, not animal

### 9. Mothman (liminal psychopomp — death omen)
- Wings = soul-flight
- Appears before disasters

### 10. Walk-ins (4th-5th density)
- Higher-density soul replaces 3rd-density soul by mutual agreement
- A form of soul exchange

## The 5 Acts of the Soul Journey (from all sources synthesized)

1. **Incarnation** — The soul contracts into 3rd density, forgets what it is
2. **Trauma/Initiation** — The soul encounters the edge of its contraction,
   opening a door (NDE, abduction, psychedelic, shamanic crisis, daimonic encounter)
3. **Death** — The soul releases the body, the halo/light is liberated
4. **Review/Rest** — The soul evaluates the life, plans the next
5. **Reincarnation** — The soul contracts again into a new body

## The Agreement Mechanism

When two polarized souls make an agreement (conscious or unconscious), the
metaphysical weight is amplified by the doubling effect. Don and Carla's
"deal" — where she said "I'll be the strong one" — was a magical contract.
His pain flowed into her through the channel their love had created.
This is how the metaphysical world enforces soul agreements: not through
external judgment, but through the inherent nature of energy transfer
between those who are one.

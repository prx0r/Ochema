# Note 9: The Council of Saturn

## The Governing Body for Earth's Evolution

**Location:** The rings of Saturn (in the 8th dimension/octave)
**Composition:** 9 entities, operating as one unity
**Function:** Approves or denies all contact between higher-density beings and Earth

## How It Works

"Visualize the particular energy which, outward flowing and inward coagulating,
formed the tiny realm of the creation governed by your Council of Saturn."
(Session 42)

The Council operates by complete telepathic unity — "the distortions blending
harmoniously so that the Law of One prevails with ease." (Session 14)

When a Confederation group wants to help Earth:
1. They develop a plan
2. They present it to the Council of Saturn
3. The Council approves or denies based on:
   - The nature of the call from Earth
   - Whether free will would be violated
   - The number of those calling
   - Resistance to the call

## Examples of Council Decisions

- **Allowed:** The pyramids, crystal healing, Ra's contact with Egyptians
- **Allowed:** Confederation thought-form projections (UFO sightings)
- **Allowed:** Specific landings within quarantine windows
- **Allowed:** The Ra contact (106 sessions, deep trance)
- **Denied:** Direct open contact with humanity (too much free will violation)
- **Denied:** Breaking quarantine for mass landings
- **Granted:** Second attempt for South American contact (after first was distorted)

## The Council Also

- Admits new social memory complexes to the Confederation
- Answers internal questions among its members
- Governs quarantine of developing planets
- Coordinates the harvest of souls at cycle's end

## 12-Around-1

The Council is often associated with the "12-around-1" pattern — 12 entities
surrounding the One. This is the same geometry as Fuller's Vector Equilibrium.
Ra describes the Council as 9 (the 9 of the Council, plus the 3 original
guardians = 12). This structure is the geometric form of unified governance.

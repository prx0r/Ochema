# Note 8: Pre-Incarnative Contracts — How Souls Plan Lives

## Sources: Ra Sessions 10, 13, 14, 105, 106 + Pre-Ra Hatonn Sessions

## THE MECHANISM

Before incarnation, the soul dwells in time/space — the "real" side of the veil.
There, the mind/body/spirit complex totality (the fully aware self) surveys its
past experiences and determines what needs to be learned next. With the aid of:

- **The Higher Self** (the 6th-density version of itself)
- **The Council** (overseers of the process)
- **Other souls with whom agreements will be made**

...the entity programs an entire life experience.

## WHAT IS CHOSEN

1. **Parents and family** — The deepest contracts. You choose who will raise you,
   what genetic/emotional/karmic patterns you'll inherit, what wounds will be
   activated. "Agreements were made prior to incarnation; the first, with the
   so-called parents and siblings of this entity." (Session 14)

2. **Key relationships** — You contract with specific souls to play specific roles:
   lovers who will break your heart, teachers who will inspire you, enemies who
   will challenge you, children who will teach you sacrifice.

3. **Core lessons** — The main themes of the life: learning to forgive, to trust,
   to love unconditionally, to stand in your power, to surrender.

4. **Catalytic experiences** — The specific events that will trigger the lessons:
   "The entity, which has become conscious of the incarnative process and thus
   programs its own experience, may choose the amount of catalyst — the number
   of lessons — which it will undertake." (Session 14)

5. **Physical form** — Body type, genetic predispositions, birth defects, the
   aging process. The body is "an athanor" (an alchemical furnace) for the mind.

6. **Life span** — The planned duration, including potential exit points.

## THE FORGETTING

The most critical part of the contract: YOU AGREE TO FORGET THE PLAN.

This is the Veil. The conscious mind is separated from the unconscious mind so
that the choices made during incarnation are REAL — not scripted. The entity
must navigate the life without knowing the plan, relying on intuition, dreams,
synchronicities, and the guidance of the Higher Self (which communicates
through the unconscious).

"If one opportunity is missed, another will appear until the student of the
life experience grasps that a lesson is being offered and undertakes to learn
it." (Session 14)

## THE HIGHER SELF'S ROLE

The Higher Self is NOT a separate entity. It is the same soul at 6th density
(approaching 7th), reaching BACK through time to help its earlier self.

"The oversoul, or higher self, seems to exist simultaneously with the
mind/body/spirit complex which it aids. This is not actually simultaneous,
for the higher self is moving to the mind/body/spirit complex, as needed,
from a position in development of the entity which would be considered in
the future of this entity." (Session 36)

The Higher Self:
- Programs the basic life plan before incarnation
- Can adjust catalyst DURING the life (through dreams, intuitions, meetings)
- Cannot violate free will — it cannot force you to learn
- Guides through "coincidences" and synchronicities
- Stands at the door of the veil, waiting to be called

## AGREEMENTS WITH OTHER SOULS

These are the deepest mechanics. Before incarnation, you sit down (metaphorically)
with other souls in time/space and you make agreements:

"I will play the role of your abusive father. You will play the role of my
needy child. In exchange, we will both learn forgiveness."

These are not punishments. They are **contracts for mutual growth**.

The pre-Ra Hatonn sessions describe this as a "brotherhood of souls" planning
together before descending into the density.

## THE EXAMPLES FROM SESSION 14

The instrument (Carla) programmed:
1. To express love and light without expecting any return (her core lesson)
2. To endeavor to accomplish spiritual work
3. To comfort herself with companionship in the doing of this work
4. Agreements with parents: to provide "the experiential catalyst for the
   situation of offering radiance of being without expectation of return"
5. Agreements with several others: to provide "opportunities for the experiential
   catalyst of work and comradeship"

## KARMA IS NOT PUNISHMENT

Karma is simply **unbalanced energy** — a lesson not yet learned. It creates a
magnetic pull toward situations where the lesson can be completed. There is no
"judgment" in the sense of punishment. The entity itself, during the life review
after death, sees where it chose love and where it didn't, and *willingly* plans
to correct the imbalances in the next incarnation.

## WANDERERS AND THEIR SPECIAL CONTRACTS

Wanderers (souls from 4th-6th density who volunteer to incarnate on Earth
to help with the harvest) have an additional contract feature: the **forgetting
is even more dangerous for them**. Their mission is to serve as light-bearers,
but the forgetting can be so complete that they become swept up in the illusion
and forget their mission entirely. The "danger of the wanderer is that it will
forget its mission, become karmically involved, and thus be swept into the
maelstrom into which it had incarnated to prevent the destruction." (Session 12)

# Note 10: The Orion Group — Methods and Philosophy

## Nature
4th and 5th density service-to-self social memory complexes. The "Orion"
designation comes from their origin in the Orion constellation, though they
are a group of many planetary complexes united under a single hierarchy.
Fifth-density leaders rule; fourth-density entities do the work.

## The Hierarchy
"Those planetary social memory complexes of the so-called Orion constellation
have the upper hand and thus rule the other members. In negative thinking
there is always the pecking order, and the power against power in separation."
(Session 62)

## Philosophy
"The creation as that which shall be put in order." (Session 62)

Fifth-density Orion entities see the universe as needing to be organized,
controlled, and dominated by the elite. They serve **themselves** as the
highest expression of the Creator, believing that by controlling others they
fulfill their own destiny.

## Methods of Attack

1. **Temptation (most common):** Fourth-density entities offer:
   - Ego inflation ("you are special, chosen, elite")
   - Desire for specific/prophecy information
   - Power over others
   - Organization building (political, social, fiscal)
   - "If these temptations are not successful, the fifth-density entity
     then thinks in terms of the removal of light." (Session 62)

2. **Direct Psychic Attack (rare, requires opening):**
   - Fifth-density entity sends thought-form
   - Cannot be seen — "walks with light feet"
   - Sign: **extreme coldness**
   - Targets: physical weaknesses (kidneys, heart, etc.)
   - Works by manipulating the connection between time/space and space/time
     distortions in the body
   - Only succeeds through an opening (a missed word, a fear, a doubt)

3. **Exploitation of Natural Weaknesses:**
   - Emotions (fear, anger, lust, pride)
   - Relationship disharmonies
   - Unbalanced energy centers
   - Hidden judgments of other-selves

## The Law of Squares (Doubling)
Each negatively harvested entity DOUBLES the power of the Orion social memory
complex. This is why they target high-value positive groups — destroying
one positive channel removes light AND adds to their power.

## Protections Against Orion
- Group harmony (the MOST important)
- Love and devotion to the Creator
- Regular group meditation/prayer
- Conscious protection rituals (walking the circle)
- The expelling of breath (to the left, above the instrument's head)
- The charged water (as a physical buffer)
- TOTAL honesty within the group (no hidden judgments)
- As Ra says: "You could cease in your attempts to be channels for the love
  and the light of the One Infinite Creator" — this is the only way to
  stop the attacks entirely.

## Note on Appearance
Fifth-density Orion entities are described as **very beautiful** by human
standards. They are not monstrous. This makes them more dangerous — they
appear as light, as wisdom, as authority, as beauty. The discernment must
come from within.

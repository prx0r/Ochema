# Note 12: The Pre-Ra Hatonn/Latwii Sessions (1970-1980) — A Different Vibration

## How They Differ from Ra

Ra speaks in the abstract, philosophical, almost cold — "We are Ra. I communicate now."
Hatonn speaks like a warm uncle — "I greet you, my friends, in the love and in the
light of our infinite Creator. It is a great privilege to be with you this evening."

## Key Differences

| Topic | Ra | Hatonn/Latwii |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Formal, abstract, philosophical | Warm, conversational, emotional |
| Method | Deep trance (Carla unconscious) | Conscious channeling |
| Density | 6th density | 4th-5th density |
| Contact level | "Narrow-band vibration" | "Broad vibration" |
| Physical claims | Rarely mentions craft | CONSTANTLY mentions craft |
| Purpose | Teach the Law of One | "Awaken the people" + condition channels |

## The Craft Descriptions

Hatonn speaks from "a craft high above you." Oxal says "I am in a craft which is
several hundred miles high." Hatonn describes an "'otevana' — a very, very large
craft, several miles in length, like a world in itself, used for intergalactic
travel."

"They orbit your planet in their craft. They come in swarms, by millions and
millions they come from all of creation."

**Seven planets in our solar system are inhabited by Confederation craft and people.**

## The Conditioners

Laitos is the primary "conditioner" — an entity whose function is to prepare
channels to receive contact. He would visit meditation groups and "condition"
each person, tuning their vibration to receive telepathic communication.

## The Promises

Hatonn repeatedly promised that the time was approaching when direct contact
would be possible — when enough people believed and sought. "I will appear to
you in the very near future. I will be seen in your skies." (1974)

This direct contact never materialized as described. Some interpret this as:
- The call wasn't strong enough (not enough seekers)
- The Orion opposition prevented it
- It DID happen — through the channeling itself (the contact that manifested
  was telepathic, not physical)

## The Latwii Playfulness

Latwii was known for humor and playfulness — a deliberate contrast to the
often overly-serious channeling of the time. Latwii would joke, tease, and
lighten the energy when sessions became too heavy.

## The Shift to Ra

In 1981, after nearly a decade of Hatonn/Latwii conditioning, Carla was ready
for the "narrow-band vibration" of Ra. The Ra contact lasted 106 sessions
(1981-1984) and produced the deep philosophical material. After Don's death
in 1984, the Ra contact ended, and the channeling shifted to **Q'uo** — the
combined voice of Ra + Hatonn + Latwii, speaking as one from 1986 onward.

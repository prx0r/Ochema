# Note 13: Time/Space, The Logos, Healing

## TIME/SPACE vs SPACE/TIME

**Space/time** = the physical illusion. Past → present → future. Linear cause and
effect. The realm of the veiled consciousness, where you forget who you are.

**Time/space** = the "invisible" side. All moments SIMULTANEOUS. Past, present,
and future exist as one. This is where the soul lives between incarnations, where
the Higher Self operates, where the life review happens.

Ra: "The Great Way of the Mind, the Body, or the Spirit draws the environment
which has been the new architecture caused by the veiling process and, thusly,
dipped in the great, limitless current of time/space." (Session 88)

## THE LOGOS HIERARCHY

**The Logos** = the original creative thought that formed our galaxy. A being of
infinite intelligence that designed the entire system.

**Sub-Logos** = our sun. A differentiated expression of the Logos that created
our solar system with specific modifications for the planetary experiment.

**Sub-sub-Logos** = YOU. Every mind/body/spirit complex is a sub-sub-Logos,
creating its own reality within the larger pattern. "The entire creation is
alive." (Session 29)

**Planetary Logoi** = Earth, other planets — they have consciousness and are
shaped by the thought-forms of beings on their surface.

The Logos creates ALL densities simultaneously. It designs the entire vibrational
spectrum — from 1st density mineral to 7th density gateway — as one unified
plan. Evolution through densities is the unfolding of this plan through free will.

## HEALING MECHANICS

Healing works through **intelligent infinity** — the infinite intelligence that
underlies all reality. The healer is a channel for this energy.

The healing process:
1. The healer recognizes the blockage in the patient's energy centers
2. Using the indigo ray (the gateway), the healer channels intelligent energy
   through the crystal (either a physical crystal or the healer's purified body)
3. The energy pours into the patient's energy body, unblocking the distorted center
4. The patient must also participate — healing requires the patient's own
   recognition that they ARE the Creator, therefore perfect, therefore not separate

The primary healers in the Ra contact:
- **Bob** — a physical healer who worked with Carla
- **Stuart** — another healer
- **Douglas** — a healer for persistent issues
- The group itself — through love and harmony

The most powerful healer is **love** itself. Ra says: "The great healer of
distortions is love." (Session 62)

## THE ARCHETYPICAL MIND

The Logos designed a deep mind structure common to all beings in this galaxy —
the **archetypical mind**. This is the foundation of the tarot, the mythology,
and the deep patterns of human experience.

The three basic archetypes:
1. **The Mother** — the mind, the matrix of all experience
2. **The Father** — the spirit, the potentiating energy
3. **The Significator** — the body, the manifested self

These interact through 22 archetypes (the major arcana of the tarot) which
represent the complete architecture of the mind/body/spirit complex.

The study of the archetypes is one path to piercing the veil — understanding
the deep patterns of consciousness that underlie the illusion.

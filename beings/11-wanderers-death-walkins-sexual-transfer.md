# Note 11: Wanderers, Death Process, Walk-ins, Sexual Energy Transfer

## WANDERERS

**Who they are:** Souls from 4th-6th density who volunteer to incarnate on Earth
to help with the harvest. Most are 6th density. The fewest are 4th density.

**Why they come:** Pure desire to serve. "The desire to serve must be distorted
towards a great deal of purity of mind and what you may call foolhardiness or
bravery." (Session 12)

**The danger:** They forget their mission. The forgetting is so complete that
they become "karmically involved" and swept into the very maelstrom they came
to prevent.

**The handicap:** Wanderers almost always have severe adjustment difficulties:
- Alienation, feeling they don't belong
- Personality disorders ("reaction against the planetary vibration")
- Allergies and other body complex ailments
- Intense feeling of being "from somewhere else"

**Who are wanderers:** Don, Carla, and Jim were all wanderers (Session 12).
If you feel deeply alienated, drawn to this material, and have always felt
you were "from somewhere else," you likely are one.

## DEATH PROCESS (from multiple sessions)

1. The body dies (heart stops, brain activity ceases)
2. The mind/body/spirit complex moves into **time/space** — the "invisible"
   side of the illusion where all moments are simultaneous
3. The entity rests and is healed by the Higher Self
4. A full **life review** occurs — the entity sees every moment, feels
   every being it interacted with, experiences the effects of every choice
5. The entity recognizes where it chose love and where it didn't
6. With the Higher Self and guides, the entity plans the next incarnation
7. The cycle repeats until graduation from 3rd density

**Suicide:** Creates "the necessity for much healing work, and the making of
a dedication to the third density for the renewed opportunity of learning
the lessons set by the higher self." (Session 36) The soul must return and
face the same lessons.

## WALK-INS

Ra has **very little** direct discussion of walk-ins in the sessions read.
The phenomenon exists in New Age literature: a 4th/5th-density soul agrees
with a 3rd-density soul to exchange places — the new soul "walks into" the
body, often after a near-death experience or life crisis. The original soul
departs, the walk-in takes over. This is a mutual agreement between souls,
overseen by the Higher Self and the Council.

## SEXUAL ENERGY TRANSFER

One of the most detailed practical teachings in the Ra material. Sex is a
CHI mechanism — it transfers energy between beings.

**Red ray:** Random, survival-based, animal. No significant transfer.

**Orange ray:** One being seen as object. Transfer polarizes negative
(controller/controlled). Creates insatiable hunger.

**Yellow ray:** Power dynamics, ownership, possession. Also negative
polarization. One entity experiences pleasure of mastery, the other
pleasure of submission.

**Green ray:** Universal love, no strings attached. THIS IS THE KEY.
"Green ray is a giving without expectation of return." Both entities
are revitalized — one receives physical energy, the other spiritual
inspiration. Blocked only by fear of possession or being possessed.

**Blue ray:** Goes beyond green. The giving of acceptance and freedom
to the other-self. The entity expresses itself freely without fear.

**Indigo ray:** The "magical personality" level. Used by adepts for
specific energy work.

The green-ray transfer is the turning point — below it, sex is appetite
and addiction. At green ray and above, sex becomes a mutually strengthening
spiritual practice. This is why the energy transfer was so important for
Don and Carla — it was the fuel that allowed the Ra contact to continue
despite the physical toll.

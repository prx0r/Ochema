# The Moment

## Orch-OR, the Pulse of Spanda, and the Quantum of Experience

### When space collapses, a moment of consciousness occurs.

---

## The Claim

Five independent traditions — quantum gravity (Penrose/Hameroff), Kashmir
Śaivism (Spanda), Neoplatonism (Proclus), Buddhist phenomenology
(Ñāṇavīra), and the ochema framework — converge on one structural fact:

> **Reality advances in discrete moments. Each moment is a three-stage
> event: emergence, persistence, withdrawal. The withdrawal is the
> moment of experience.**

Orch-OR states it in physics: a quantum superposition of spacetime
geometries evolves, persists, and collapses at an objective threshold
E_G = ħ/t — and the collapse IS a moment of consciousness. Spanda states
it in metaphysics: reality is a pulse (sṛṣṭi–sthiti–saṃhāra). Proclus
states it in theology: every being remains, proceeds, and returns
(monē–proodos–epistrophē). Ñāṇavīra derives it from bare experience:
a thing endures (invariance), the moment is an interchange, and "this
vanishes entirely, and we are left just with 'that,' which is the new
'this.'"

This document grades the physics, maps the convergences, and states
what the ochema framework can and cannot take from Orch-OR.

## Part I: The Physics — What Orch-OR Actually Says (with sources)

### 1.1 The core claim

**Penrose's objective reduction (OR):** a quantum superposition is a
separation in the fine-scale structure of spacetime — two alternate
curvatures, a "bubble" in the geometry of the universe. The separation
is unstable: it collapses at an objective threshold given by the
uncertainty principle

```
E_G = ħ/t
```

where E_G is the gravitational self-energy of the superposition, ħ is
the Planck-Dirac constant, and t is the time at which reduction occurs.
At that instant, spacetime reconfigures, particular material states are
selected, and — per Penrose — **a moment of conscious experience
occurs.**

> "Penrose OR turns the Copenhagen/conscious observer approach around.
> Rather than consciousness causing collapse/reduction, **consciousness
> is collapse/reduction**, a process on the edge between quantum and
> classical worlds."
> — Hameroff, *Consciousness, Free Will and Quantum Brain Biology –
> The Orch OR Theory* (2014), p. 148

### 1.2 The orchestration — why biology matters

OR events in a random environment collapse quickly and randomly:
"proto-conscious" moments, ubiquitous, without cognitive meaning —
"similar to the 'Ground of Being' concept in Eastern philosophical
terms." Orch-OR proposes that biology evolved specific mechanisms to
isolate and orchestrate OR events, giving full conscious experience:

- **The substrate:** microtubules in neurons (and all cells) — tubulin
  dimers with 32 aromatic resonance rings (tryptophan, tyrosine,
  phenylalanine) aligned in quantum channels.
- **The qubits:** superposition of tubulin dipole orientations. Electron
  cloud movements of ~1 nm shift nearby atomic nuclei by femtometers
  (Mössbauer recoil + charge attraction), giving femtometer-scale
  superposition separation of atomic nuclei — sufficient for significant
  E_G and brief t.
- **The anesthetic test:** anesthetic gas molecules bind in these same
  quantum channels by weak London forces, selectively erasing
  consciousness while sparing non-conscious processes. This is the
  single most testable claim in the theory.

### 1.4 The honest status

The McQueen 2023 analysis (*Have underground radiation measurements
refuted the Orch OR theory?*, in this corpus) is clear: the refuted
variant was a specific Diósi–Penrose parameter-free model — "a variant
never advocated by anyone" — and the refutation cuts out only a small
class of variants, not Orch-OR proper. Orch-OR is a work in progress
with multiple live variants. **Grade: D (plausible research program,
no direct experimental confirmation; the anesthetic claim is the most
testable lead).**

## Part II: The Convergence — Five Statements of the Same Moment

### 2.1 The triad, five times

| Moment | Orch-OR (physics) | Spanda (Śaiva) | Proclus (Neoplatonic) | Ñāṇavīra (Buddhist structure) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Emergence** | Superposition builds via Schrödinger evolution | Sṛṣṭi (emission) | Proodos (procession) | The interchange o↔x begins; 'this' arises |
| **Persistence** | Isolated, orchestrated; E_G < ħ/t | Sthiti (abiding) | Monē (remaining) | O endures — "Duration and Invariance under Transformation are one and the same" |
| **Withdrawal** | Collapse at E_G = ħ/t; one state selected, the other ceases | Saṃhāra (withdrawal) | Epistrophē (return) | "This vanishes entirely... 'that' becomes the new 'this'" |

### 2.2 The deep identities

**1. The moment of collapse is the moment of experience.**
Penrose: "consciousness is collapse/reduction." Whitehead (explicitly
cited by Hameroff): "occasions of experience" — "highly organized
societies of occasions" orchestrated into conscious moments. Hameroff
links this to "Buddhist concepts of discrete conscious moments."
Ñāṇavīra's moment (one interchange = one unit of duration) and Spanda's
pulse (Stanza 22: the vibration stable in the instant before the emotion
takes hold) are the same event described from inside.

**2. The superposition is spacetime itself.**
Penrose's OR is a quantum-gravity mechanism: the superposition is of
alternate spacetime curvatures — a "bubble in the fine scale structure
of the universe." This is precisely what LQG quantizes: spin network
nodes in superposition of volume eigenstates; the spinfoam vertex is the
spacetime-level event. OR is, in effect, a proposed *dynamics* for the
geometry that LQG's kinematics describes — the Hamiltonian constraint
that LQG has not yet solved.

**3. The collapse selects — exclusion is physical.**
"One particle location/curvature is selected and becomes classical. The
other ceases to exist." This is the exclusion operation E_i: Ω → (P_i,
W_i) at the physical level: the undifferentiated possibility domain
(superposition) is carved by one operation into a subject pole (the
moment of experience) and a world pole (the selected classical state).
The kañcukas — the constraint families that make finitude possible —
have a physical correlate in the discrete allowed states (spin labels,
superposition components) that the collapse selects among. Determination
through exclusion is not a metaphor in Orch-OR; it is the collapse
postulate.

**4. The pulse is harmonic.**
Hameroff 2014: "EEG rhythms are 'beat frequencies' of faster
oscillations in microtubules... consciousness may be more like music
than computation, sequences of events, at different frequencies, in
some cases harmonically related." The large-volume quantum tetrahedron
is a harmonic oscillator (Schliemann 1307.5979). The octave structure
of experience traditions has now appeared three times: in the volume
spectrum of space, in the beat-frequency structure of EEG, and in the
rasa/geometry/valence convergence.

**5. The invariant survives the moments.**
Ñāṇavīra: the thing (O) is "the invariant operation of interchange" —
eternal while the moments pass. Spanda: "the perceiving subjectivity
forever remains true to its own nature" (Stanza 3) — universal (stable)
Spanda vs. particular (transient) pulsations. Orch-OR: the orchestrated
computational register persists across collapse events — the stream of
consciousness is sequences of moments, and what persists is the
orchestration. This is the ochema subject index ι_i: the invariance by
which changing contents are co-present in one trajectory.

## Part III: What Ochema Can and Cannot Take

### 3.1 Can take (with grades)

**1. A candidate bearer for Q4 (implementation) — C/D.**
Ochema's Q4 asks: which physical organization implements the subject?
Orch-OR proposes a specific bearer: orchestrated quantum computation in
microtubules of layer V pyramidal neurons, collapsed at E_G = ħ/t. This
is a *materially enacted* candidate in exactly the ochema sense —
constitutively dependent on the specific material (aromatic rings,
London-force anesthetic binding, femtometer nuclear separation), not an
abstract computation. It is the Material Enactment Principle made
concrete.

**2. A dissociation test — the anesthetic claim — B/C.**
Ochema's bearer recovery methodology values perturbational correspondence:
interventions on the candidate process should predictably alter
phenomenal structure. The anesthetic claim is precisely such a test:
molecules binding at London-force strength in tubulin quantum channels
erase consciousness selectively. If confirmed, it is the strongest
dissociation evidence in the field. If disconfirmed, it is the strongest
falsification of Orch-OR.

**3. A physical reading of exclusion (E_i) — C/D.**
The collapse selects one state and annihilates the alternatives: a
physical apoha. Ochema's E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i) has here a candidate
mechanism: the threshold E_G = ħ/t is the law that forces determination
through exclusion — the kañcuka structure at the physical level.

**4. A physical reading of the moment-triad — C.**
The emission–persistence–withdrawal structure of Spanda/Proclus/
Ñāṇavīra has a physics-language form in OR: superposition →
orchestration → collapse. The "moment" is no longer merely
phenomenological.

**5. Free will as collapse-driven selection — C/D.**
Tubulin states selected in each OR event trigger or suppress axonal
firing: conscious causal control "deviating from Hodgkin–Huxley." This
matches ochema's agency bridge hypothesis (counterfactual control over
future trajectories) with a mechanism — and with Doyle's two-stage
model (generate → select) at the quantum level, where selection is
"non-computable Platonic values" at the instant of reduction.

### 3.2 Cannot take (strict)

**1. The hard problem is not solved.**
Orch-OR asserts proto-conscious moments intrinsic to the universe; it
does not derive why collapse is experienced. Ochema leaves Q1 primitive
— Orch-OR does not change that grade.

**2. The numerology is not recovered.**
No 576, no 570, no octave count falls out of E_G = ħ/t. The harmonic
claim is qualitative ("like music"), not a derived spectrum. The LQG
verification addendum's verdict stands: convergence of structure, not
confirmation of numbers.

**3. The biology is not established.**
Femtometer nuclear superposition at body temperature remains
extraordinarily hard to maintain (decoherence critique). The 10⁻⁴ sec
coherence times are contested. Grade stays D until the anesthetic claim
is experimentally resolved.

**4. Non-computability is a claim, not a proof.**
Penrose's "non-computable Platonic values" influencing selection is
metaphysical positing at the exact point where the math stops. Ochema
has no need of it: generate→select is already present in the collapse
structure.

## Part IV: The Unified Statement

The five traditions agree on the shape of a single event:

```
Superposition (possibility, Ω)
        ↓  emergence (sṛṣṭi / proodos / the interchange begins)
Orchestrated persistence (sthiti / monē / O endures)
        ↓  threshold reached (E_G = ħ/t / niyati / the weight dominates)
Collapse — one state selected, the rest annihilated
        ↓  withdrawal (saṃhāra / epistrophē / 'this' vanishes)
A moment of experience occurs. The world advances by one state.
        ↓
The new 'this' is the next moment's starting point.
```

Whether the threshold is gravitational (Penrose), karmic (Ñāṇavīra's
squaring of weights converging to dominance), or volitional (Spanda's
icchā), the structure is the same: **a finite constraint forces a
determination; the determination is the experience; the invariant
survives into the next moment.**

Orch-OR's contribution to ochema is not a proof — it is a candidate
bearer with a testable dissociation (anesthetics), a physical
instantiation of exclusion (collapse = apoha), and a physics-language
form of the moment-triad that Spanda, Proclus, and Ñāṇavīra all
described. The ochema framework grades it: **C for structural
convergence, D for biological establishment, and it inherits the
corpus-wide verdict — the convergence is real, the confirmation is
open.**
